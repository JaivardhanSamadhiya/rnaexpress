# RNAddress versus the PV-Care benchmark

## Decision

**NOT PV-CARE LEVEL in its current evidence state.**

RNAddress has a clear inverse-design concept, substantial public intervention data, and unusually careful computational controls. It does not have the successful locked algorithmic result, independent external validation, original wet-lab experiment, integrated prototype, or user evaluation needed for a comparable overall ceiling.

| Dimension | RNAddress current evidence | PV-Care benchmark described in the project specification | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conceptual novelty | Clear requested localization change to minimal cis-edit framing | Clear proactive context-aware capability | RNAddress concept is compelling but empirically unproven. |
| Simple explanatory power | “Where should this RNA go, and what one-base change can route it?” | Proactive assistance based on sensed state | Potentially comparable as a concept. |
| Algorithmic novelty | Pairwise intervention ranking and explicit edit representations; no locked advantage over forward search | Custom SFR-Net within a larger system | RNAddress is weaker. |
| Technical sophistication | Truth-safe reconstruction, multiple model families, exhaustive candidate ranking, freeze and hostile audit | Learning method plus sensing, wearable, environment, and LLM integration | Different strengths; RNAddress lacks system integration. |
| Evidence quality | Strict parent-held-out historical intervention evaluation; three-parent locked test | Original experimental and user evidence | RNAddress is rigorous but materially narrower. |
| Evidence scale | 4,395 clean exhaustive N-zip SNVs; Mikl and external resources carefully separated | More than 30-person EEG experiment, 60-person screening, 20-participant evaluation | RNAddress has more molecular rows but far fewer independent primary units. Row count is not a substitute for participants or parents. |
| External validation | Not performed because the internal gate failed | Multiple experimental validation layers | Decisive RNAddress deficit. |
| Biological/social significance | Minimal RNA routing could matter scientifically | Direct human assistance application | RNAddress has high potential, not demonstrated impact. |
| System integration | Deliberately not built after gate failure | Wearable prototype and integrated pipeline | RNAddress is far behind. |
| Demonstrability | Historical mutation rankings can be visualized, but no successful locked examples justify a polished demo | Intuitive working proactive-assistance scenario | RNAddress is weaker. |
| Reproducibility | Strong: source hashes, manifests, frozen predictions, model hash, fixed seeds, tests | Not assessed beyond the supplied comparison facts | RNAddress has a credible strength here. |
| Competition presentation | Honest negative internal result with a strong audit trail | Global-gold-winning complete story | Not comparable. |

## Why the ceiling fell

The prompt required the custom inverse method to beat a strong forward predictor plus exhaustive search on unseen parents. It led development but lost that exact comparison on the untouched internal lock. The result also has only three locked parent units and broad parent-bootstrap uncertainty. Proceeding to the sealed external benchmark or building a polished product would convert a science-gated project into an outcome-seeking one.

## What remains valuable

The repository is a reproducible feasibility study with a reusable truth-safe intervention benchmark, a documented example of why mutation-row counts must not replace parent-level inference, and evidence that generic forward search is a serious baseline for RNA intervention recommendation. Those are meaningful research outputs, but they do not justify PV-Care equivalence or a global-gold-ceiling claim.
