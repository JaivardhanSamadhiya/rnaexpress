All intervals condition on fixed training and choices. Composition-class
resampling accounts for within-class neighborhood overlap, but does not supply
independent experiments or full training uncertainty. NRS is a normalized log2
abundance-ratio score, not a cellular percentage or clinical effect.

One earlier interpretation is specifically invalid: a positive symmetric mean
of increase/decrease changes alone cannot establish improvement over the parent
in both directions, because the parent score cancels algebraically. The later
direction-specific audit supplies separate evidence for positive means; it does
not repair that inference or eliminate individual failures.

## Possible contribution and its boundary

The candidate contribution is the **evaluation package**: a precisely defined
composition-controlled forced-choice task, competitive simple models, cluster-aware
descriptive uncertainty, constituent-read reconstruction, arithmetic replay and
explicit per-decision failures. The exhaustive library measurements and broad
motif/prediction concepts belong to the source study and prior literature.
No exact-composition decision benchmark was located in the bounded inspected
article/README, but absence from inspected text is not proven novelty.
