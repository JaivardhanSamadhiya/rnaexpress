# Secondary cross-experiment assessment of fixed configuration policies

AI-authored specification, 23 September 2026 local date. This follow-up is
explicitly motivated by the failed primary sequence-interaction gate. In that
experiment the two common intron-pattern baselines improved over random on
development. This is a new secondary hypothesis, not promotion of the failed
interaction model or reopening of its confirmation set.

Question: do both fixed, sequence-independent configuration policies (quadratic
and categorical pattern) retain benefit in the separate 8-hour transient
transfection experiment? Only Supplementary Table 3 transfection_2 rows labelled
exactly `8 hr` may be read. Exclude every original confirmation GA pattern from
all access. The other condition is labelled `16 hr` in the sheet despite a
24-hour description in the paper/Contents; exclude it entirely. Doxycycline,
CLK, stability and all original confirmation outcomes remain closed.

Use the same four-intron, same-designed-mature-RNA panel rule. Metadata gives
28 eligible GA patterns/panels after excluding reserved GA patterns, with at
least five distinct genotypes per panel. Source and target construct genotypes
may overlap; this tests transfer across experimental time/collection, not unseen
genotype or gene generalization. Barcodes are aggregated within genotype/replicate.

Refit the original four models on precisely the original training rows using
their already-selected alphas. No tuning, new source labels, choice reversal,
or use of development values. Freeze predictions for all admitted 8-hour candidates
before reading their outcomes. CPM ratio agreement must pass. Use all three
replicates and the original normalized regret/tie rules. Report all four models,
exact random regret 0.5, and 5,000 paired GA-pattern bootstrap intervals with
seed 20260923. These intervals are descriptive and omit training uncertainty.

Support for this secondary hypothesis requires BOTH quadratic and categorical
pattern policies to improve mean regret over random by >=0.05, each lower
95% descriptive bound >0, and each of their three replicate gains positive,
with >=20 eligible GA patterns. Report every model whether successful or not.
No additional data become accessible based on this result.

Site-specific intron identity/length and placement remain bundled; this does
not identify a causal mechanism, establish a novel algorithm, or imply cross-gene
validity. The author already analyzed intron-position associations. Any novelty
claim must concern a demonstrably distinct decision benchmark and survive
further prior-art scrutiny.
