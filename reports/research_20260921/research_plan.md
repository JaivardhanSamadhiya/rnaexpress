# RNAddress computational research sprint

Started 2026-09-21 UTC (2026-09-20 America/Los_Angeles).

Authorization: explore, download free public data, and run local computational
experiments. No spending, cloud purchases, wet lab, scheduled tasks, messages to
authors, or changes to sealed-data permissions. All historical verdicts stand.

This is a new exploratory research workspace, not Mechanism-v6, a replacement
evaluation, or a claim of a successful selector. An STS outcome or complete
novelty cannot be guaranteed. A positive finding must survive relevant controls;
all attempted experiments, including negatives, will be retained.

## Work order

1. Audit the mathematical interpretation of the reliability/ceiling and random
   projection controls, using synthetic data and existing nonsealed evidence.
   Do not fit or re-score an old selector.
2. Inventory new public resources: SRLE-seq (HRA016642), Arora et al. neuronal
   MPRA (GSE183192), and SEERS (HRA008408). Verify actual downloadable assets,
   sequence/replicate coverage, units, provenance and overlap before admission.
   Literature-described results are already known; label any related hypotheses
   informed by those results rather than outcome-naive discoveries.
3. Choose a small number of new-data experiments only after feasibility checks.
   Write exact estimands, partitions, nulls, stopping rules and analysis code
   before scoring. Keep an independent confirmation set unopened where possible.

## Initial outcome-free audit specification

- Reproduce the v5 84-by-84 Gaussian projection on synthetic features. Measure
  matrix rank and reconstruction error; demonstrate that information is retained.
  This does not establish better prediction and cannot change any frozen gate.
- Demonstrate that replacing missing uncertainty with zero creates perfect
  observed-label rankings for that subset, not a measurement-noise bound.
- Inventory uncertainty semantics, grouping and repeated sequence/context rows
  in the two explicitly hash-pinned development tables. No model fitting.
- Inspect the existing v3 diagnostic JSON. Its noisy-oracle and fitted-model
  diagnostics must not be described as proven bounds on all possible methods.
- Record initial/final tracked working-tree hashes. Preserve user modifications.

## Resource admission criteria

Record URLs, retrieval times, SHA-256 and available license information. Never
execute downloaded scripts/notebooks or deserialize external pickle models.
Download small metadata/processed resources before large raw archives. Inspect
archive member paths before extracting; restrict outputs to this new namespace.
Distinguish real measured edits from random sequence libraries and in-silico
mutagenesis. Distinguish new assays from independent genes/sequence families.

## Candidate scientific directions (not success claims)

- Replicate-aware localization selection: establish a measurement model with
  consistent signs, units, WT covariance and count-depth effects.
- Context-calibrated selection: determine a learning curve for a fixed calibration
  budget on genuinely separate data. Compare simple calibration-only baselines.
- Composition-controlled sequence ordering: ask whether sequence order adds
  predictive information beyond exact nucleotide counts on an exhaustive small
  library, with replicate validation. This alone is not unseen-parent transfer.

## One-month allocation

Days 1-4: integrity/measurement audit, literature comparison and resource admission.
Days 5-12: at most two well-specified exploratory new-data experiments; retain all.
Days 13-20: freeze one candidate method and independent validation, if justified.
Days 21-30: robustness, complete reproduction and a clearly attributed report.
If no method qualifies, report a rigorous methodological or measurement finding.

## Boundaries

The existing FinalShot preservation mismatch remains unresolved: new work must
not treat the current checkout as a hash-clean historical replay environment.
