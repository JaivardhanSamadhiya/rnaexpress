# Faraway interpretation correction and bounded diagnostics

AI-authored, 23 September 2026 local date, after both reported evaluations.
The frozen specifications and results remain unchanged. This addendum corrects
their interpretation; it does not retrospectively alter either gate.

The phrase "identical designed mature RNA" in the original specifications is
too broad. The 1,703-nt reconstruction is the designed reporter insert. The
primary article's Methods, "Reporter gene and plasmid construction", state that
each reporter has a unique barcode in its expressed 3′ UTR. The correct claim
is **matched reconstructed exon sequence in the analyzed insert, shared encoded
protein, and four introns**, with different expressed barcodes. Full transcript
identity is not established. An insert-only sequence predictor ties by design;
this does not establish an impossibility result for full-transcript predictors.
Barcode effects and genotype–barcode associations remain possible confounders.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09568-w
The cached primary article and official code are retained with download receipts.
The original paper already examines intron density, individual intron positions,
and RNA multivalency. Neither the biological role of splicing nor intron-position
effects are new discoveries here.

The separate 8-hour hypothesis passed its frozen numerical criteria. It was
selected after the primary development failure and remains a secondary result
from the same study and protein context. The original interaction gate failed.
No reserved outcome becomes accessible.

Before computing further diagnostics, fix the following bounded analysis:

1. Reconstruct the design for every admitted 8-hour genotype from ordered
   fragments; check panel insert identity, and quantify genotype/GA overlap with
   the original source and development sets. Count independent barcode IDs within
   each genotype; do not compare numeric barcode IDs across sheets as identities.
2. Use only the already-opened development candidate rows and the frozen 8-hour
   candidate rows. Report both selection directions separately, absolute log2
   nucleus/cytoplasm improvement over uniform choice, and all four fixed models.
   Reproduce the original aggregate regrets before writing any diagnostic result.
3. Report all replicate-pair Spearman correlations within panels and the
   leave-one-replicate selection diagnostic. These measure repeatability, not
   a formal ceiling or independent biological validation.
4. For the two fixed common-pattern policies, permute their scores over the union
   of admitted eight-bit intron patterns 5,000 times (seed 20260924), using the
   SAME permutation in every GA panel and replicate. This preserves dependence
   from recurring intron configurations. Report one-sided Monte Carlo tail areas
   and null quantiles as post-hoc diagnostics; do not redefine the passed
   secondary criterion or call them confirmatory p-values. No refitting occurs.
