# Known cellular and reporter context: feasibility review

Prepared 7 October 2026 while the separate frozen structure/encoder extraction proceeds. This is a literature and metadata review only. No expression values, new localization outcomes, protected outcomes, full-core features, or model fits were acquired or analyzed. No frozen structure source was changed, no money was spent, and no external contact was made.

## Conclusion

An independently measured trans-context input is computationally feasible, particularly for CAD versus Neuro-2a. It could let a conditional model distinguish identical sequences assayed in different cells. It is not yet established that expression differences explain the project's disagreements. Reporter context is a separate requirement: all 80 Moffatt GEO samples are CAD cells, so a single CAD expression signature cannot explain GFP-versus-Firefly ordering reversals. Sparse independent biological contexts and mismatched reference cultures limit any claim of generalization.

The existing postfit diagnostic measured gene-macro agreement of approximately .578 for effect signs and .534 for candidate ordering between matched Mikl CAD/Neuro-2a edits. Those descriptive results motivate this hypothesis; sampling noise, shared references, and assay artifacts remain alternatives. They do not demonstrate a trans-RBP mechanism. The already exposed assays would remain development data after any future context-conditioned fit.

## Historical approach already attempted

The underlying idea is **not new to this project**. The frozen 2 September FinalShot study already used GSE67828, with three soma and three neurite replicates per CAD/Neuro-2a cell line. It resolved 98 eligible human RBPNet channels to mouse loci using a pinned MGI mapping and mm9 RefGene coordinates, then interacted each channel's binding summaries with an equal-compartment average of median log1p-FPKM. It included crossed-cell transfer, context-vector swapping and a trans-interaction knockout. The recorded Gate H comparison failed: M2's mean rank improvement was +0.00357 and regret improvement −0.00169; M3's were +0.00389 and +0.00120, below its fixed retention rule. See [the frozen trans-context report](D:/rnaexpress/reports/finalshot_trans_context.md) and [the historical source builder](D:/rnaexpress/src/audit/build_finalshot_trans_context.py).

Consequently, obtaining the same public profiles and repeating broad expression-weighted RBPNet inputs would not be a new route. A future compact motif/structure interaction hypothesis would differ in representation, dimensionality, soma-only reference definition, and addition of independently sourced HEK293T/astrocyte context. Each change needs to be declared before fitting and tested against matched controls. Soma-only abundance is a new biological assumption, not a demonstrated repair of the old failure. The existing six soma files and their pinned hashes can be reused after provenance verification; downloading them again is unnecessary. No expression-value table or historic fitted archive was opened in this feasibility review.

## Public expression resources, in priority order

1. **CAD and Neuro-2a: GSE67828 wild-type soma profiles.** Public series metadata identifies three Neuro-2a soma samples, GSM1656708–GSM1656710, and three CAD soma samples, GSM1656714–GSM1656716. The [individual Neuro-2a sample](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM1656708) lists an 821.2 KB processed FPKM file; individual files avoid the full 71 MB series archive. The author's 2016 cultures used 24 h serum deprivation, whereas Mikl uses reduced-serum differentiation. The profiles use mm9/Cufflinks and require gene-versus-isoform identifier checks. Select only these six unperturbed soma profiles for a fixed RBP panel; do not compute neurite/soma ratios or use MBNL perturbation outcomes. Soma is a proxy for the cellular expression state, not a complete whole-cell measurement. [Official series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE67828), [author methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4798900/).

2. **CAD culture-state sensitivity: GSE291553 and CaltechDATA.** The newer study contrasts whole-CAD differentiation states. The [final peer-reviewed publication](https://www.micropublication.org/journals/biology/micropub-biology-001574/) appeared 21 April 2025; the earlier PMC11952321 record is a preprint version. The [author's 2.7 MB processed workbook](https://data.caltech.edu/records/ws0pp-h2661) is openly downloadable under CC BY 4.0. Five-day serum-free/ITS differentiation differs from the reporter cultures. DESeq2 baseMean or a differential-effect table alone cannot be substituted for condition-specific absolute expression. Inspect column definitions before admitting a signature; use this study to assess reference-state sensitivity rather than choose whichever state gives the best localization result. [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE291553).

3. **Astrocyte proxy: GSE52564 purified cortical astrocytes.** The [official series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE52564) lists Astrocyte1/2,GSM1269903/GSM1269904, and a 14.3 MB processed XLS archive. Author-described cells are pooled P7 Aldh1l1-EGFP mice, rather than the project's P21 in vivo assay. Purification and developmental age can alter the apparent trans-state. This is a transparent species/cell-class proxy, not an exact matched astrocyte signature.

4. **Astrocyte translation-state sensitivity: GSE74456.** The [Sakers primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5441704/) links this accession and a 1.9 MB CSV containing twelve samples' CPM values. A future reader could admit whole-cortex astrocyte TRAP columns only after verifying exact sample identities and age; exclude SN/PAP contrasts, localization lists, enrichment statistics and motif-derived outcomes. TRAP reports ribosome-associated RNA, not total RNA or RBP protein availability. Its age/state is not certified here as P21. [Official series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE74456).

5. **HEK293T: small processed wild-type profiles.** [GSE135505](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE135505) is public and lists wild-type RNA-seq replicates GSM4013201/2 and HELZ-null replicates GSM4013203/4. Its 286.5 KB unfiltered read-count matrix provides a practical candidate; admit only the two wild-type columns, never the knockout contrast or DE table. This independent laboratory/stock/culture is not the SRLE transfected experiment. The author source and column/sample provenance must be checked before extraction.

6. **HEK293T fallback: SG-NEx.** The [author repository](https://github.com/GoekeLab/sg-nex-data) documents a HEK293T native-RNA sample and a broader public reference-transcriptome collection. The [official open-data registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/sgnex/) specifies CC BY-NC 4.0 and account-free unsigned access. Prefer an existing processed quantification if verified; raw/BAM processing is unnecessary for a small six-RBP screen. HEK293T-specific replicate coverage and matching short-read quantifications require sample-level verification; the statement that the core cancer lines have three replicates must not be transferred automatically to every extended HEK293T sample.

GEO is free to access without an account and places no repository-level reuse restriction, while retaining submitter-rights and data-quality caveats. Public deposit does not certify experimental correctness. [GEO access policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html), [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html). No expression file was downloaded during this review, and no paid compute is needed for the proposed processed-file route.

## Biological grounding and a bounded future signature

A six-gene exploratory panel could be fixed from primary mechanisms before reading signature values: **CNBP, MBNL1, MBNL2, QKI, HNRNPK, KHDRBS1**. This is a future design proposal, not an implemented or outcome-selected feature set.

- CNBP recruits a KIF1C transport complex to GA-rich cargo in tested protrusion-localization systems. That does not establish CNBP as mediator of every admitted assay. [Primary mechanism](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12002053/).
- MBNL1/2 have direct perturbation evidence for neurite localization in CAD and primary cortical neurons. Their expression can reflect differentiation state. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4798900/).
- QKI motifs have functional evidence in an astrocyte reporter system; QKI's splice isoforms can differ in intracellular role, so total gene RNA is a limited proxy. [Sakers study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5441704/).
- HNRNPK recognizes nuclear-enrichment elements whose architecture and associated factors matter. A C-rich motif or high HNRNPK expression alone does not certify retention of a six-mer HBB construct. [Primary SIRLOIN study](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature25757), [architecture study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8204871/).
- KHDRBS1 is a mechanistic candidate suggested by the exposed astrocyte motif analysis, not demonstrated causal occupancy in the assay. [Astrocyte source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13142395/).

Use stable gene identifiers and pin an Ensembl release. For mouse-to-human transfer, retain verified high-confidence one-to-one orthologues; do not convert symbols by capitalizing them or arbitrarily select among paralogues. Protein orthology does not establish identical RBP concentration, isoforms, modification, binding occupancy or transport function. [Official orthology documentation](https://www.ensembl.org/info/docs/compara/homology_method.html), [REST homology endpoint](https://rest.ensembl.org/documentation/info/homology_symbol).

FPKM, CPM, raw counts and TRAP abundance are not interchangeable absolute units. A prospectively defined within-profile gene-expression percentile over a common mapped gene universe is one possible scale, with explicit missingness and replicate spread. It reduces unit dependence but does not remove batch, age or purification effects. Future admission should require interpretable per-sample values for every panel member, or record an explicit unsupported/missing state; missing measurements must never be called zero expression.

ENCODE's large standardized eCLIP collection is primarily K562/HepG2, not CAD, Neuro-2a, P21 astrocytes or the SRLE HEK293T experiment. Its binding preferences can ground sequence features, but its peaks are not matched trans-context measurements for this project. [ENCODE primary resource](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2077-3).

## What a context model would actually need

Simply appending a constant cell vector to a linear candidate ranker cannot change within-context ordering: the cell-only term cancels when comparing two edits in the same context. The model requires a predefined sequence-by-context interaction or a genuinely conditional utility function. Six RBP values multiplied by a small, prespecified motif-delta block could supply continuous interactions, with the unconditioned motif block retained as a matched comparator. Expression-weighted motifs are still binding hypotheses, not measured binding probabilities.

A lower-cost metadata-only alternative could predeclare six bits known before observing an outcome: projection endpoint, neural lineage, in-vivo environment, CAD model, Neuro-2a model and Firefly CDS. Interact these with the same five raw local motif/composition deltas, giving 30 bounded conditional columns. This alternative has no external-expression download requirement, but does not mechanistically explain CAD/Neuro-2a differences. Endpoint, lineage and in-vivo flags are heavily confounded with source; a categorical target cell absent from all training sources has no estimable target-specific coefficient. Other reporters and unknown contexts must use an explicit common fallback, not inferred target calibration.

## Falsifiable next steps and limits

1. Certify sample identities, units, gene/isoform mapping and non-outcome column boundaries for the six CAD/Neuro-2a soma profiles and two HEK293T wild-type profiles. Select one transparent astrocyte proxy with a declared mismatch, or leave that context unsupported. Pin files/hashes and panel/normalization before any signature-versus-localization comparison.
2. Freeze a context-interaction hypothesis and matched unconditioned/metadata-only controls. Keep original whole-assay exclusion, exact-allele/component purging and source-only selection. Add held-gene tests of same-edit CAD/Neuro-2a ordering and held-cell stress tests; never use matched target outcomes to calibrate the target context. Shuffle or exchange externally measured cell signatures as a software/information control, with the permutation rule fixed before fitting.
3. Require improvement beyond the matched controls and stability to the declared reference-state sensitivity. Benefit that follows arbitrary source identifiers, vanishes under culture-matched sensitivity, or does not reduce cross-cell ambiguity weakens the trans-context hypothesis. Moffatt reporter discordance requires reporter-sequence/processing information or an explicit uncertainty limit in addition to any cell signature.

No small collection of reference RNA-seq profiles can prove a universal transport mechanism. Expression of an RBP's mRNA does not measure active protein concentration, modifications or compartmental occupancy. The main unresolved gaps are experiment-matched trans-state measurements, complete reporter processing/context, and an independent compatible confirmation. Protected data and frozen gates remain untouched; this review authorizes neither their opening nor automatic supervised execution.
