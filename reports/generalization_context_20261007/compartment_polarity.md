# Fixed compartment-polarity hypothesis: feasibility and historical audit

Prepared 7 October 2026. This is a retrospective protocol/literature review and a future hypothesis proposal. No model, feature matrix, new localization outcome, protected data or favorable sign search was run. The frozen six-track structure/encoder comparison remains unchanged.

## Recommendation

A **separate, limited fixed-polarity development test is reasonable**, because the old experiments tested a different orientation. It is not a correction to a failed experiment and not proof that nuclear export and distal transport share one scalar mechanism. Its biological premise is weak enough that it should remain a matched, falsifiable alternative rather than replace the current endpoint convention. Context-conditioned or endpoint-specific utilities are mechanistically more defensible if resources permit.

Define a prospective cytoplasmic-routing utility `u(sequence, context)` and a known endpoint multiplier `s`: `+1` for the admitted projection enrichment endpoints and `−1` for nuclear/cytoplasmic enrichment. Train pair preferences against `s × (effect_i − effect_j)` and return the assay-oriented score `s × u`. Neither multiplier is inferred from source identity, observed correlations or held-out labels. This reverses the nuclear training order as well as the inference score; merely flipping SRLE predictions from the existing pooled model would not test the proposed shared training constraint.

## Biological support and why it can fail

For a nuclear/cytoplasmic **log ratio**, algebraically changing `log(N/C)` to `log(C/N)` gives exactly the opposite sign. This establishes the nuclear endpoint's orientation; it does not establish its equivalence to neurite/soma enrichment. HNRNPK/SIRLOIN experiments support a sequence-sensitive nuclear accumulation mechanism, while CNBP/KIF1C experiments support motor recruitment and long directed movements in the cytoplasm. These distinct mechanisms make shared information plausible but do not establish a common monotone axis. [Primary SIRLOIN study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6047738/), [primary CNBP/KIF1C study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12002053/).

The denominator matters. Let cytoplasmic RNA be `C`, distal RNA be `D = pC`, and cytoplasmic soma RNA be `S = (1−p)C`. Then `D/S = p/(1−p)`. Increasing export and thus `C` while keeping the distal fraction `p` fixed does **not** increase this projection ratio. Soma measurements can also contain nuclear RNA, and astrocyte SN/cortex-input includes different operational fractions, so this simple identity is an illustrative limit rather than a literal assay reconstruction. Cytoplasmic availability is necessary for distal cytoplasmic transport, but it need not be the rate-limiting determinant of enrichment.

Conversely, greater nuclear accumulation can reflect retention, import, processing or compartment-specific decay. A primary semi-permeabilized-cell study shows HNRNPK/import-receptor-dependent SIRLOIN nuclear import and stress-granule behavior; it cannot be collapsed automatically into one export-rate scalar. [Primary nuclear-import study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11073023/). The motif architecture, cell state and reporter processing remain part of the mechanism. The polarity hypothesis therefore predicts **partial shared ranking information**, not equal physical fluxes or transferable raw effect sizes.

## Requirements for a later frozen test

Use the identical admitted core roster, exact alleles, corrected sequence input, base features, outcome-blind pair roster, component purging, source-only nested selection and penalties as an unflipped comparator. Predeclare the two endpoint multipliers, the assay-to-endpoint metadata mapping, and how both requested selection directions are converted back to the assay's measured convention. Retain every source and both directions. Do not search alternative signs or admit extra training data to just one arm.

Minimum meaningful comparisons are the matched unflipped shared ranker and the polarity ranker. If exposed SIRLOIN auxiliary rows are added, add and purge exactly the same rows in both arms, and report that augmentation as a separate factor. An endpoint-specific control addresses the possibility that neither common orientation is appropriate. The current gate and past failures remain unchanged; any future gate must be declared before execution and must not substitute for the historical one.

Require improvement across sources, including the held nuclear source, with no material projection harm and source-only selection. With only one core nuclear reporter and two astrocyte genes, even a positive result would be exposed development evidence, not broad independent generalization. A gain solely attributable to correcting nuclear score orientation would support that convention for this benchmark; it would not establish a novel universal biological mechanism. Failure or heterogeneous gain would weaken the single-axis constraint and favor explicit endpoint/context modeling.
