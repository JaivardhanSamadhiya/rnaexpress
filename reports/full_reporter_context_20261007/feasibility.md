## Mikl: retained DNA constants are a causal-isolation gap

The independent metadata audit found different published synthesis cores for WT and motif-mutant scanning constructs: WT prefix `CGAAATGGGCCGCATTGC`, suffix `CACTGCGGCTGATGACGA`; mutant prefix `GACAGATGCGCCGTGGAT`, suffix `AGCCACCCGATCCAATGC`. Each synthesized 198-nt record also includes a 12-nt barcode. The documented author PCR adds restriction tails around the complete 198-nt oligo. Manufacturer cleavage rules SgsI/AscI `GG^CGCGCC` and SdaI/SbfI `CCTGCA^GG` retain both entire 18-nt cores and the barcode in the assembled DNA. [SgsI primary manufacturer specification](https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/ER1891), [SdaI specification](https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/ER1191), [primary Mikl study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9561380/).

This certifies a conditional local cloned-DNA construction, not a 198-nt mature RNA. Exact final master-cassette sequence, reporter TSS, complete RT-primer map, splice/cleavage products and barcode-to-reference aggregation are still needed before encoding whole expressed transcripts. Targeted reporter sequencing examples support retention near the insert, but their specific RNA-versus-DNA sample identity was not uniquely certified in that bounded audit.

The public author lookup's field called `barcode` is 14 nt, comprising the last two primer-core bases plus the true 12-nt barcode. An illustrative notebook also contains barcode/UMI offsets that differ from the methods description. Those examples do not certify the exact production count pipeline and are not evidence of a numerical count error. A future sequence-to-measurement reconstruction must pin the appropriate field definition and aggregation rather than assuming a name fixes the retained RNA interval.

The existing 150-nt allele differences and author numerical contrasts remain correctly reconstructed. Common WT/reference and mutant-constant additive effects cancel when comparing candidates within a parent. Nonlinear structure/context interactions and candidate-specific barcode effects can remain, however. The new metadata must not be used to call every full WT-to-mutant reporter an isolated 1–6-base intervention, or to invalidate within-parent ranking solely from its larger construct difference. Current 150-nt experiments stay frozen.

## Moffatt and remaining admission requirements

The companion audit certifies the published 260-nt variable inserts and identical 20-nt synthesized arms in the inspected mutation FASTA records. It does not yet uniquely certify which complete GFP/Firefly cassette, restriction products, TSS or cleavage/processing states correspond to each measured reporter. A generic GFP or firefly coding reference cannot resolve experiment-specific linkers, backbone changes or transcript boundaries.

Before a full-context representation test is admitted, require an author deposited final construct or otherwise uniquely constrained sequence; exact insert orientation/junctions and retained barcode records; reporter-to-assay/clone mapping; a declared RNA species with supported start, splice and cleavage boundaries; and explicit treatment of multiple barcode/WT records. An RNA processing mixture cannot be represented as one conveniently chosen mature transcript. A source-specific partial DNA reconstruction should remain labeled as such.

## A future matched test, with a clear stopping rule

Once those links are certified outcome-free, compare a fixed full-context representation with the same representation on the already certified local insert/context, using identical rows, solver, source-only selection and global gene/original-allele exclusions. Preserve the existing raw, static-pretraining and structure-accessibility controls. Use declared per-barcode sequence features and the original author aggregation units; do not average sequences or choose the most favorable splice product. A context benefit would be evidence about predictive information under those construct assumptions, not proof of a transport mechanism.

For SRLE, precursor and canonically spliced reference designs could first serve as an outcome-free sensitivity calculation, explicitly provisional and not evaluated as a measured full-transcript reconstruction. They cannot enter a confirmatory or production comparison while final clone/end/processing evidence is missing. For Mikl, local retained-DNA context is similarly a provenance target until RNA identity and barcode aggregation are resolved. **The present audit therefore supports better provenance and restrained future hypotheses, not a newly certified full-RNA feature route.**
