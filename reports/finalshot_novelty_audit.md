# FinalShot novelty refresh — 9 September 2026

## Scope and conclusion

This is a bounded literature refresh, not an exhaustive proof of priority. Search
results and accessible primary papers were reviewed on 9 September 2026. No new
data or representation was admitted to the frozen experiment. No protected
Astrocyte material or N-zip outcomes were sought or accessed for this review.

The defensible contribution is the **specific decision-focused, grouped transfer
and mechanism-falsification benchmark for RNA-localization interventions**. It is
not the invention of RBP-binding prediction, variant deltas, RNA language models,
latent phenotype/measurement separation, or cell-aware RBP modeling. The current
results do not establish a successful universal RNA intervention compiler.

matches is not evidence that the scientific idea has never been attempted.

## Closest verified precedents

| Prior work | Established overlap | Boundary relative to FinalShot |
|---|---|---|
| [RBPNet, Horlacher et al., Genome Biology 2023](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-023-03015-7) | Sequence-to-CLIP-signal learning and variant-sensitive RBP predictions | Its pretrained outputs are reused, not invented here. Binding-profile prediction is distinct from choosing localization edits on new biological parents. |
| [Parnet preprint, Moyon et al., August 2026](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.08.743506v1) and [paper repository](https://github.com/marsico-lab/parnet--paper) | CLIP-based RNA representation learning with multi-RBP output space | Strong prior art for the representation premise. The paper-matching checkpoint failed the frozen resource audit; this refresh does not reverse that exclusion. Search indexing exposed the preprint; a subsequent direct page open failed. |
| [DeepLocRNA, Wang et al., Bioinformatics 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae065) | Deep-learning prediction of RNA subcellular localization | Localization prediction itself is not novel. FinalShot instead evaluates within-parent intervention decisions and cross-assay transfer against edit geometry. |
| [BRIDGE, Wang et al., Nature Communications, 15 May 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73086-0) | RNA sequence/structure integration, cell-context RBP interaction modeling, and genetic-variant effects; the paper reports transfer to unseen contexts | RBP-conditioned variant effects and context transfer cannot be claimed as firsts. BRIDGE's target and required modalities differ from the certified mouse neurite/soma intervention benchmark. |
| [MAVE-NN, Tareen et al., Genome Biology, 15 April 2022](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-022-02661-7) | Joint inference of genotype-to-latent-phenotype and assay measurement processes | The conceptual separation used by M3 is established prior art. FinalShot tests a restricted shared-score/multi-assay application, not a new general latent-phenotype framework. |
| [RNA localization mechanisms transcend cell morphology, eLife](https://elifesciences.org/articles/80040) | Experimental evidence about shared RNA-localization mechanisms across cellular morphologies | Biological transferability is an existing research question. Such findings motivate, but do not validate, a universal edit-selection model. |

GenerRNA and RBPscan also appeared in the broad search. GenerRNA's indexed
primary article describes RNA generation including RBP-binding tasks; RBPscan
describes quantitative in-vivo RBP interaction measurement. These are relevant
adjacent areas, not evidence that FinalShot's combined benchmark has already
been performed. GenerRNA's PMC page returned a browser challenge on direct
open, so detailed architectural comparisons were not inferred from it.

## Claims supported and not supported

Supported as a description of this work: a reproducible evaluation connecting
frozen human RBP-binding representations to mouse RNA-localization intervention
ranking across three certified assay sources, with biological-unit holdouts,
direction-specific decisions, strong geometry comparators, nested model
selection, source/cell/reporter transfers, and mechanism-breaking controls.

Potentially distinctive as a combination, but not proven first: treating
cross-study intervention choice as the target and requiring RBP-identity and
matched-delta necessity before interpreting gains biologically. Originality of
that exact combination remains qualified; independent review may find closer
prior art.

Not supported: a novel foundation model; first RNA inverse design; first
RBP-aware variant scorer; discovery of a universal localization grammar;
validated TDP-43 binding prediction from a model without a TARDBP checkpoint;
clinical or therapeutic readiness; or PV-CARE-level success merely because
some averaged metrics improve.

## Consequence for the submission

The mechanism controls retain substantial performance after intended information
is disrupted. Therefore the useful scientific result may be a well-controlled
negative finding: predictive transfer alone did not establish the intended RBP
mechanism or reliable small-edit control. Report favorable and unfavorable
results together. Do not replace the frozen model with a newly available model
or search for a post-hoc favorable subgroup to manufacture a novelty claim.

This refresh does not authorize RNAddress-Adapt or prospective Phase C work;
either requires a separately defined protocol. The final scientific verdict
remains linked to completion of the required computational audit.
