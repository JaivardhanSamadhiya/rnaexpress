## Splits and models

Original sequence holdout uses only the original training pool. Whole-composition holdout additionally excludes the held class. Primary purged composition holdout retains original training sequences only if the A/C/G/T count vector has L1 distance **>4** from the held class. This removes every sequence within two substitutions of any full-class member and guarantees Levenshtein distance ≥3. Exact audits find nearest Hamming and Levenshtein 3 for all 855 scored six-mers. Original test sequences never enter any training fold. Training sizes are 578–2,827 for purged folds; no split was relaxed. The same 855 sequences and 1,744 candidate links are evaluated under all three schemes. Biological-context holdout is unavailable.

Published score is the admitted author NRS(log2FC); edit target is candidate minus parent. Constituent NRS1/NRS2 were previously calculated as `log2((nuclear_count+0.5)/(nuclear_library_total+2048)) - log2((cytoplasmic_count+0.5)/(cytoplasmic_library_total+2048))`; their saved sequence scores are differenced identically. Position-additive uses 24 one-hot features; position-pair adds all 240 two-position indicators, total 264. Composition predictions are training-only class means, with a train-only standardized mono-count Ridge fallback for unseen classes. Order models predict residuals after training class means and add the same baseline. No tuning or new architecture search occurred. ## Predictions, decisions and uncertainty

Effect prediction is frozen score(candidate) minus frozen score(parent). For same-composition pairs the nuisance baseline cancels. The 2-mer effect reconstructs as sum of delta counts times coefficient/scale; intercept and centering cancel. Centering all 16 count weights is a presentation gauge since their count differences sum to zero. Coefficient medians/IQRs describe 70 overlapping models, not independent uncertainty. The separately documented boundary algebra addendum was recorded after the original controls and before its calculation; it does not fit or deploy any new model.

Primary effect losses/correlations are row-weighted, using complete class sums for 2,000 paired composition-class resamples, NumPy default_rng seed **20260926**, integer draws of shape (2000, number of classes). Relative MSE reduction is `1 - sum(model_squared_error)/sum(baseline_squared_error)`. Original effect and score estimands are separate. R² uses measured variance. Strict direction accuracy excludes true ties and gives no credit for predicted ties; separate tie-half ranking metrics are not relabeled as strict accuracy. Numerical zero tolerance is 1e-12.

Candidate sets are scored for both requested directions. Select argmax(direction × prediction), breaking exact prediction ties lexically by candidate; do not add the unchanged parent or abstention. Normalized regret is `(max(direction × measured_delta) - direction × selected_delta) / range(direction × measured_delta)`. Uniform is the exact mean over candidates, including joint wrong-both outcomes; it is not one sampled random choice or a product of marginal probabilities. Directional success requires signed effect >1e-12. Means average anchors/directions within composition class, then classes equally; paired class resampling gives descriptive percentile intervals. No refitting occurs inside resampling. Covariance across overlapping fits and uncertainty across biological contexts are not captured.

Median/quantile/ECDF summaries give each parent/direction equal mass, and uniform uses its exact candidate probability mixture. This differs from class-balanced means and is labeled. Top-three best recovery is evaluated only for >3 candidates: 308 decisions in 30 classes. Other decision metrics have 1,184 decisions in 60 classes. Metric-specific denominators are exported.

Held-out labels are never shuffled into training. All controls use alpha=10 and training-only scaling. All 2,040 fits and every null result are retained, with no seed retries. Feature-name permutations with matched weights are an identity, not a null.

Distance bins are exact observed integer levels; intervals are displayed only with ≥5 classes. Continuous associations, all failure strata and all coefficient feature associations remain descriptive. Fixed magnitude bins are ≤0.1, 0.1–0.25, 0.25–0.5, >0.5. Constituent replication is one experiment; replicate-selection benchmarks are not independent validation or an absolute ceiling. Wrong-both partitions distinguish a both-correct alternative, an alternative merely avoiding wrong-both, and every candidate being wrong-both.

Confidence signals are top-two prediction margin, desired-direction predicted effect, negative |2mer−3mer| disagreement on the selected candidate, negative worse-endpoint nearest 2-mer L1 distance, and negative maximum endpoint count-range exceedance count. For each of 2mer/kmer123, retain ceil(N × coverage) at 100/80/60/40/20%; ties parent then direction ascending. Outcomes do not set these rankings/cutoffs, but the exposed risk curves remain exploratory; none is deployed. Predetermined examples are nearest to median average constituent regret among correct-both and wrong-both decisions, separately by direction, ties parent then selected sequence. All candidates are shown even when the published aggregate disagrees with constituents.
