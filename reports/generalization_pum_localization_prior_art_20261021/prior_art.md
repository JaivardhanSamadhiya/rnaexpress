# Pumilio localization prior-art check — 21 October 2026

Pumilio-dependent neuronal RNA localization is established biology. The proposed contribution remains a controlled computational test of small-edit selection in excluded genes/cells; a PRELib binding result cannot substitute for that result.

[Grzejda et al., 2025, *Pumilio differentially binds to mRNA 3′ UTR isoforms to regulate localization of synaptic proteins*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s44319-025-00401-z) was published on 21 February 2025 ([PMID 39984683](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39984683/); DOI 10.1038/s44319-025-00401-z). Its abstract reports preferential Pum binding to long neuronal UTR isoforms associated with the soma, synaptic enrichment of shorter isoforms, and isoform mislocalization in Pum-deficient fly neurons. Thus neither Pum-dependent localization nor isoform-specific somatic retention is a new claim available to this project.

The reviewed methods use endogenous tagged fly Pum with cross-linking/immunopurification and 3′-end sequencing, native synaptosome/input isoform comparisons, and soma/neurite separation of cultured primary fly neurons carrying Pum loss-of-function alleles. These protein perturbations and native isoform differences are not certified RNA 1–6 nt substitution menus. The abstract/methods do not establish an excluded-gene/cell edit-ranking predictor. Drosophila Pum observations also do not calibrate human PUM2 energetic parameters in the project's reporter/cell contexts.

| Claim | Existing primary evidence and project boundary |
|---|---|
| Pum binding can affect neuronal RNA localization | Also established in mammalian neurons by [Martínez 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6895424/), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2019.08.035. A localization direction prior is not proof of universal edit effects. |
| Register ensembles, flips and coupling predict PUM affinity | Established by [Jarmoskaite 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6645366/). Our independently implemented fixed allele scores are a representation/reproducibility test, not new binding physics. |
| Thermodynamic predictions relate to mutant cellular binding | [Farberov–Ulitsky 2024](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/52/21/13402/7848851) already compared predicted occupancy with mutant reporter binding. Our PRELib development analysis tests fixed-score transfer and controls; it is not localization validation. |
| Folding can couple multiple PUM binding events | Established by [Becker 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6521599/). Two-site geometry or later joint opening does not demonstrate a new mechanism or cellular occupancy. |
