# Finite pooled-association held-lineage development experiment
Status: POST-EXPOSURE DEVELOPMENT ONLY. PRELibB raw-count summaries, instability diagnostics and published results have already been examined. This source cannot become untouched confirmation. Unknown input/IP lysate pairing does not prevent the declared pooled estimator, but prevents matched-replicate claims. No labels enter fitting until independently replayed fixed features, metadata overlap audit, complete source/ancestry/alias certificates and a committed prefit manifest pass.

## Cohort and endpoint
All4,054 exact one-base substitutions, all16 WT/mPRE backgrounds and all7 nominal RNA lineages remain, including all HIAT1 zeros. No count-dependent filters/weights. Each source ID is anchored to its Excel row and exact DNA; external source aliases represent the same measurements, not extra constructs. Entire NORAD and WT/mPRE families stay together. Block if cross-lineage identical DNA is found; do not silently change fold count. Primary pooled PUM2 association target: mutant-minus-parent of mean log2(PUM2_IP raw+0.5) minus mean log2(Input raw+0.5). This is association enrichment, not equilibrium affinity or localization.

## Small comparison and source-only selection
Three fitted arms: Kmer (84 normalized overlapping1-3mer mutant-minus-parent counts); Kmer+PUM2 (85, add fixed released_csv delta_logZ); Kmer+duplicate (85, duplicate the predeclared TGT3mer delta). All arms use exactly the same weighted ridge estimator and splits. Intercept=0, no centering; training-only weighted RMS scaling, zero scales replaced by1. Thus identical parent self-features predict0. Objective sum(w*(y-Xb)^2)+lambda*sum(b^2), weights sum1. Each training lineage has equal weight, its backgrounds equal weight, then its candidates equal weight. Lambda grid[0.005,0.05,0.5]; choose smallest lineage-balanced inner MAE, ties within1e-12 choose larger lambda, then fixed arm name. Seven outer whole-lineage holds, six inner whole-lineage holds per outer. This yields378 inner fits and21 outer fits, no outer-label selection. Train-only span residual of the PUM2 scalar versus kmer features is recorded; a zero residual supports only regularization, not additional sequence information. Duplicate control does not prove mechanism.

## Metrics, fixed comparisons and sensitivity
Primary MAE: candidate mean within background, background mean within lineage, equal7-lineage macro. Report all7 lineages separately. Zero-effect predictor is descriptive. Secondary average-tie Spearman within backgrounds and normalized stronger/weaker extreme-choice regret, including stable source-ID tie choices, all menus and flat-truth/flat-prediction counts. Both-direction regret is averaged; truth-span<=1e-12 is zero regret with explicit flat-menu reporting. Fixed published delta_logZ / ln(2) receives rank/concordance evaluation without fitting or choosing an operator. Report all4 fixed operators; released_csv is primary, remaining3 cannot rescue it.

Use the same outer predictions against18 frozen target views: for each pseudocount0.5 and1, pooled meanIP/meanInput, each of2IP columns with meanInput, each of2Input columns with meanIP, and all4 crossed singleIP/singleInput contrasts. These are correlated estimator sensitivities, not biological replicate probabilities. PUM1 targets are descriptive specificity views only, with no refit, selection or rescue. Library representation and normalization limitations remain. No PUM-to-localization sign is inferred.

## Provisional robustness criterion
Kmer+PUM2 must reduce primary macro MAE by>=5% against BOTH Kmer and Kmer+duplicate; improve at least5/7 lineages against each; retain strictly positive macro MAE gain after dropping each comparison's best lineage; and retain strictly positive macro gain against both controls under every one of the17 alternative target views. Primary paired lineage bootstrap95% lower bounds must exceed0 for both controls (10,000 resamples of7 whole lineages, seed20261009; linear interpolated percentiles). No mutant-level independent bootstrap. Report all metrics even if the criterion fails. A pass supports predictive usefulness of this fixed prior for this particular pooled association endpoint, not independent biological confirmation, affinity, causality, localization transfer, novelty certification or an STS outcome.
