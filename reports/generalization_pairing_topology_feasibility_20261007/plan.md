# Pairing-partner redistribution: source-only feasibility, 7 October 2026

This is a narrow representation audit and defer recommendation, not a production/prefit protocol. No project allele, outcome, feature array, model or prediction is read here; no native fold, download or fit is performed. Earlier frozen conclusions remain unchanged. Utility is unknown.

## Direct deficiency in the current representation

The frozen NEXT `route_structure.ensemble` forms the symmetric global base-pair probability matrix P internally, then retains only nucleotide unpaired probability u, pairing entropy H, expected absolute partner distance d and ensemble energy. `structure_cache` stores those three vectors and energy, not P. The eleven supervised ensemble columns summarize u/H/d at changed sites and their +/-10nt neighborhood, plus AG/GA/CCTCCC accessibility marginals. No column encodes which nucleotide pairs with which, the partner base identity/orientation, a partner-distance distribution, stem/bulge connectivity or joint occurrence of two structured elements. This follows from code, not from a target performance interpretation. Source references are pinned in the receipt.

These summaries cannot algebraically recover P. The accompanying standard-library proof constructs two arbitrary, legal single-pair ensembles over the SAME invented 15nt RNA. Each ensemble is a mixture of eight canonical/GU noncrossing one-pair structures with equal mass. All fifteen u/H/d entries are exactly identical, while nucleotide0 pairs to cytosines with total mass0.25 in one ensemble and uracils with mass0.25 in the other. Consequently identical sequence and stored summaries hide partner-base information. This demonstrates noninvertibility only: these are NOT two Turner/Boltzmann folds of that RNA and do not establish that this exact collision occurs in project alleles. Energy is not asserted equal, and NEXT11 does not use it. It is not evidence that missing partner information caused previous failures.

## Primary biological motivation and limits

Singh and colleagues' May2026 Drosophila Egl-BicD study identifies recognition of bent stem loops, particular positioned base pairs and cooperation between RNA elements. Its bend measurement uses three-dimensional structural coordinates; pair marginals do not determine that geometry. Some recognized noncanonical pairs also fall outside ordinary canonical/GU folding. The openly accessible article has CC-BY4.0 terms. No numeric source data, coordinates, reporter outcomes or sequences were downloaded. This motivates testing retained secondary-structure information, not transplantation of a fly recognition rule into mouse/human reporter assays or a claim of measured neuronal mechanism. [Primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41594-026-01794-8).

Mammalian structured localization recognition predates this project. The Rgs4/Staufen2 study reports a long-range duplex and context-dependent RNP/localization behavior, including a duplex not recovered by folding its whole fragment in the cited prediction. Thus sequence truncation and trans-protein context can remain limiting even with richer predicted pairing information. Its manipulations and neuronal conditions do not supply the existing four-assay small-edit candidate menus. [Primary Rgs4 study](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/22/23/13021), [author article in PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8657808/).

## A specific future falsifiable test, not yet authorized

If root admits a later route, the narrow test would ask whether edit-induced redistribution of pairing partners adds transferable ranking information beyond the existing ordinary summaries and a matched raw-pair opportunity control. One candidate compact block is 32 changed-site pools: partner base4 x left/right2 x fixed distance bins4 (4-10,11-25,26-50,>50nt); sum Pij in each cell, average over corresponding changed indices, then mutant minus parent. These are per-edge marginals, not probabilities of stems, bulges, simultaneous elements, three-dimensional bends or occupancy. They do not encode exact absolute partner identity and are deliberately narrower than a graph/3D architecture search. A new production/prefit protocol would need to fix the entire definition, raw control, duplicate-summary/capacity control, matched strict gate and supervised orientation before extracting/fitting. This note freezes none of those choices.

Fresh ordinary global PF would be needed: the old cache cannot reconstruct this block, and no running GQ/ordinary producer may be modified to retain P. Its exact fixed available contexts would remain46nt certified HBB-vector junction for SRLE and150/190/260nt inserts elsewhere, with no invented flanks or certified full mature reporter. It would need outcome-blind synthetic BPP upper-triangle/symmetry/normalization, changed-coordinate, reversal/no-edit and full four-length checks, exact old-summary replay under the same runtime/parameters, immutable birth hashes, independent fit replay and a resource-only benchmark before admission.

## Compute and defer decision

Using only already declared length counts (742/9318/3991/4169), storing a full float64 P per18,220 allele would require5,096,996,576 payload bytes (4.747GiB), before metadata/compression; triangular float64 payload would be2,535,402,040 bytes (2.361GiB). These are arithmetic planning figures, not an inventory or compression measurement. Streaming one transient P into compact summaries avoids this disk cost. Fresh-fold timing is UNKNOWN and must be benchmarked; neither GQ two-PF timing nor an old summary cache is a new timing certificate.

Defer project extraction until current exact-feature cell-axis tests and accepted encoder tasks finish. A compact partner-distribution experiment is technically plausible on one CPU, but presently has no outcome evidence. Defer full 3D prediction, simultaneous-stem probabilities and a fly-specific feature redesign: they require different validation and are not recoverable from the old cache. The scientific claim, if a separately frozen matched experiment later passes, would remain empirical ranking improvement on the specified held contexts, not a novel structure method or established cellular transport mechanism.
