## 1. Joint motif opening: a distinct representation correction

[Li et al., RNA 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2874161/) explicitly compared probability of an entire binding site being unpaired with average/minimum individual-base accessibility. Whole-site accessibility improved binding prediction for several tested RBPs. That is binding evidence across particular proteins/species, not proof of localization-edit transfer. [Bernhart et al., 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3063221/) provide an O(n^3) algorithm for all interval opening probabilities, implemented in RNAplfold; the paper is CC BY 2.0. [Official ViennaRNA window-partition documentation](https://www.tbi.univie.ac.at/RNA/ViennaRNA/refman/partfunc/window.html) exposes interval unpaired probabilities through `PROBS_WINDOW_UP`.

Current NEXT and RBP features explicitly multiply a motif score by the arithmetic mean of per-base unpaired marginals. Source inspection found no historical producer of joint motif-window opening. For a motif interval I, use P(all bases in I unpaired), rather than mean(P(base i unpaired)). The former cannot exceed the minimum marginal; the latter can substantially overstate simultaneous opening. This difference can change mutant-minus-parent motif scores even with unchanged nucleotide composition.

A bounded proposed comparison would retain the existing 421 direct experimentally supported human PFMs (widths4–25), exact sequence boundaries, strand, scoring, four pools and outcome-blind projection. Compare existing raw specificity, existing marginal-weighted specificity and a new joint-weighted block under the same source-only estimator/grid and purges. Keep raw and marginal information controls; adding a block must improve their matched controls, rather than crediting the extra dimensions. The PFM score remains a specificity proxy, not a dissociation constant. Multiplying it by an opening probability does not produce measured occupancy.

Use already-installed ViennaRNA2.7.2; its [exact research-use terms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViennaRNA/ViennaRNA/v2.7.2/COPYING) permit free research/educational/commercial use with attribution and redistribution restrictions. No checkpoint download or new dependency should be necessary. Before any project extraction, validate the exact available Python callback and interval indexing on synthetic sequences against the independent hard-constrained global partition-function ratio exp(-(G_constrained-G_free)/(RT)). A window equal to the input length is a candidate way to preserve the existing global ensemble, **not an assumed API-equivalence**; prove it numerically before choosing it. Do not silently substitute local window/span settings.

Single-allele memory is small at input length≤260. A dense float32 table covering all18,220 alleles, all260 positions and all25 lengths has a conservative upper storage bound of473,720,000 bytes, before compression and metadata; a streaming pool implementation could avoid saving that full table. Actual CPU time and API overhead are unmeasured. Benchmark synthetic lengths46/150/190/260 and one worker before freezing a budget. Preserving all18,220 allele identities and all26,258 original rows remains mandatory. No full-reporter interpretation is gained.

## 2. G-quadruplex competition: feasible but biologically conditional

[Subramanian et al., 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3128965/) experimentally tested G-quadruplex localization elements in CaMKIIa/PSD-95 reporter contexts. [Goering et al., eLife 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32510328/) connect FMRP's RGG domain, G-quadruplex binding and neurite localization. The eLife article's [public primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7279889/) is CC BY. These mechanisms are established prior art; they do not establish shared action in SRLE nuclear retention or an astrocyte operational fraction endpoint.

Older project representations include GGAGG, G-run and G-quadruplex-like sequence summaries. They do not by themselves compute competition with ordinary stems. NEXT's inspected model-details builder does not enable G-quadruplex folding. [Lorenz et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24334379/) extend the folding dynamic program to include this competition. The [official RNAfold tutorial](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViennaRNA/ViennaRNA/master/doc/source/tutorial/RNAfold.rst) documents G-quadruplex-aware MFE and partition functions, using a simplified layer/linker-length energy model that omits linker identity/asymmetry and strand orientation effects.

A prespecified compact block could summarize mutant-minus-parent changes in G-quadruplex-aware versus standard ensemble free energy and correctly retrieved local G-quadruplex probability near edits. Retain standard folds, raw G-run/arrangement scores and no-change/reversal controls. The ordinary upper-triangle base-pair matrix is not a safe substitute for quadruplex occupancy: validate Python-accessible probability semantics before extraction. Restrict the first outcome-free probe to synthetic sequences using the existing runtime; no new learned model is required. Runtime and usable probability APIs remain admission blockers.

Do not call predicted folding actual in-cell structure. [Guo and Bartel, Science 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5367264/) found widespread unfolding of potential RNA quadruplexes in tested eukaryotic cells, although cell-state/compartment-specific exceptions remain possible. Thus a predictive gain would concern sequence folding potential under a simplified equilibrium model, not proof that quadruplex transport explains the assay. Uncertified reporter boundaries and missing protein/helicase state persist.

## 3. CAD/Neuro-2a RBP expression: public, small and already attempted

[GSE67828](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE67828) contains unperturbed triplicate soma profiles for N2A (GSM1656708–10) and CAD (GSM1656714–16), plus neurite and separate perturbation/state cohorts. It lists processed FPKM files. The [N2ASoma1 metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM1656708) lists an821.2KB file, mm9/Cufflinks2.1.1 processing and poly(A) RNA. These public official records establish free accessibility and experimental lineage; they are not an author-wide CC software/data license. [GEO reuse policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html) and [disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) should be preserved with any future acquisition, including submitter-rights caveats.

The [2016 primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4798900/) used24h serum-free differentiation. [Mikl's primary methods](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/18/10643/6717835) use reduced serum, CAD0.8% and Neuro-2a1%, rather than the identical culture/batch. This is closer than an arbitrary cell atlas, but not an experiment-matched protein-state measurement. FPKM tables require locus/isoform mapping and do not measure active RBP protein, localization or binding. Another study, [GSE200004/eLife2023](https://elifesciences.org/articles/80040), concerns epithelial apicobasal RNA-seq and does not supply a certified matched whole-transcriptome pair for these two neuronal cell lines.

The same GSE67828 files are already cached and were used before in FinalShot:98 RBPNet channels, equal-compartment averages of replicate-median log1p-FPKM, same-RBP binding interactions, crossed-cell tests, swapped context vectors and trans-block knockout. The recorded retention gate failed. Downloading the same files or presenting the same idea as a fresh discovery would be misleading.

A future compact direct-PFM/joint-opening × soma-only expression test changes representation and the reference-state assumption. It is a reassessment, not untouched evidence. It requires deterministic mouse/human gene mapping, missing-state handling, fixed scale, predeclared sample-only boundaries, no MBNL perturbation/localization contrasts, and matched unconditioned, old-proxy and signature-swap controls. Existing source hashes should be verified rather than redownloaded.

There is also an identifiability problem: cell-only columns cancel in a linear within-parent ranking; raw plus expression-weighted motif columns are collinear when fitting only one source cell. With CAD-only training, a changing target-expression vector imposes an assumed reweighting, not an independently estimated cell-response law. Two known cell profiles cannot substantiate arbitrary unseen-cell generalization. Both-cell training with gene exclusion would instead test gene generalization conditional on known cells, a separate estimand. A small feature table and linear head are CPU-feasible, but biological matching and the conditional-model interpretation need resolution before fitting.

## Independent measured intervention dataset checked

[Yergert et al., PLOS Biology2021](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3001053) provide a genuinely different in-vivo zebrafish oligodendrocyte/myelin localization system, explicit motif-deletion cloning primers and public supporting data; the article/data are described as available within the paper/supplements under CC BY. The design targets a few genes, including mbpa/eif4ebp2/fmr1, and measures MS2-associated fluorescence in sheaths/soma. This is a species, glial-lineage, developmental and reporter-system shift.

It does **not** meet the present broad small-substitution gate: motif deletions/insertions are not the unchanged-length1–6 substitution roster; a few parent genes cannot supply20 independent eligible groups, and fluorescence normalized to a control is not automatically the same paired enrichment contrast. Exact clones, edit sizes, biological replicate/larva grouping, candidate menus and overlap with existing genes require metadata certification before any future outcome access. Candidate-specific abundance and the24xMS2 reporter need explicit treatment. This could support a separately frozen, narrow indel/reporter stress test; it cannot rescue the current substitution claim or be counted as broad confirmation. No numerical supplement was opened or downloaded.

The neuronal260nt tiling paper returned by search is already the Arora experimental lineage; new windows or a re-export are not independent intervention confirmation. RNA expression MPRAs, endogenous APEX/Halo-seq atlases and isoform comparisons have different endpoints/lineage and cannot be relabeled as small-edit validation. Existing mutREL/Wen and reserved sources stay unadmitted/protected.
