Written 7 October 2026 before this generation's fits. This is a separately declared follow-up to the observed corrected-base polarity signal (macro regret about .4733 versus matched unflipped .5123). The earlier polarity experiment remains NO-GO; its protocol, results and gate are unchanged. Prior observations motivate this generation, so this is repeatedly exposed development evidence, not a fresh prospective confirmation or guaranteed novel result.

Keep the exact 26,258 original interventions, outcomes, candidate sets, original alleles and biological components. No new extraction, encoder, folding, projection, feature subset, layer, seed, context or preprocessing is selected. Carry a fixed sign through `endpoint_class` alone: projection +1 and nuclear_cytoplasmic −1. Reject unknown/missing endpoint classes, with no source-ID or effect fallback; candidate sets require one endpoint. Do not append sign to fitted features. Copy training labels and multiply them by the declared sign, fit every original sequence feature column, then multiply latent predictions by the held endpoint's declared sign. All decisions, source validation and outer evaluation use unchanged original truths and both requested increase/decrease directions. Source IDs, metadata and stored truths are never relabeled. Checkpoint label/row/configuration hashes are rechecked independently. Holding SRLE leaves projection-only training, so each aligned track must select exactly the corresponding unflipped configuration, reproduce its coefficients/scaling, and predict exactly negative scores for the nuclear endpoint. A violation triggers an audit, not favorable rescoring or tuning. No new statistical permutation experiment is added.

This tests one shared utility convention under known endpoints. Projection enrichment and nuclear/cytoplasmic enrichment do not measure one established flux: if distal D=pC and cytoplasmic soma S=(1−p)C, then D/S=p/(1−p) is independent of total cytoplasmic amount C. One nuclear source confounds endpoint with assay, cell state and reporter. Even an incremental success would be limited development evidence; it cannot establish a universal export/transport mechanism, calibrated benefit, independent confirmation or STS-level novelty.
