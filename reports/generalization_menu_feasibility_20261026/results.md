## Binary performance and exact prediction ties

For an informative two-candidate menu, two-direction normalized regret equals
one minus tie-aware ordering accuracy. The saved binary strata therefore give:

| Frozen arm | Conditional binary regret | Implied tie-aware pair accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pooled | 0.5039864420 | 0.4960135580 |
| Independent | 0.5065231277 | 0.4934768723 |
| Joint shrinkage 10 | 0.5022529829 | 0.4977470171 |

These are descriptive conditional values; there is no new stratum-specific
significance test or success claim. The failure includes binary ordering,
which is near the 0.5 ordering reference. It cannot be attributed solely to
correctly ordering middle candidates while choosing the wrong extrema of
large menus.

No arm has an exactly constant-score complete menu. This follows from saved
maximum/minimum scores and their canonical chosen IDs; no epsilon tie band was
introduced. It rules out a universal constant-score-menu explanation for this
run. It does not prove absence of individual feature collisions, good feature
support, informative score magnitude, calibration or a particular biological
cause. Tiny nonzero score spans are still nonzero under the original rule.

## Consequence for the proposed next study

For a two-candidate menu with signed correct score margin m, the proposed
menu-cost loss simplifies exactly to

`tau * log(1 + exp((1-m)/tau))`, with tau = 0.25.

Both directions give the same expression. Thus its binary effect is a shifted,
rescaled logistic loss, not a distinction between costs of several alternatives.
Any future claim about menu-level cost alignment needs a matched margin control
in addition to the original logistic baseline. Otherwise a favorable change
could simply be due to the margin/temperature change on the dominant binary
stratum. This is a design requirement identified before any new biological fit.

The new comparison core includes all three arms and tests exact binary
loss/gradient equivalence of menu-cost and margin-pair. Its plan has 120 tasks,
108 inner and 12 outer, rather than the initial two-arm draft's 80. The frozen
earlier mathematical prototype/draft remains unchanged. No 80-fit or 120-fit
biological campaign has started, and no fit authority is inferred.

For completeness, the current joint regret contributed by menus with more than
two candidates is 0.1461542798. Under the hypothetical restriction that binary
choices stay exactly fixed and every larger-menu choice becomes perfect, total
regret would be 0.3544246441. This is a restricted arithmetic bound only; it
does not establish achievability or a ceiling on other predictors/biology, and
cannot certify the original comparative gates. It does not justify filtering
out binary menus or promoting the larger-menu stratum to primary.

## Verification and limits

Four invented diagnostic tests pass. They cover unequal hierarchy masses,
conditional metrics, exact ties, the restricted bound, incomplete/duplicate
directions and inconsistent extrema. Actual reconstructed primary regrets
match the original evaluator within 1e-12 for all three arms. The original
NO-GO remains unchanged.

This diagnosis narrows the rationale for the new loss. It does not demonstrate
that changing loss will help, identify measurement noise as the cause, or make
the exposed known-cell folds independent confirmation. The richer-representation
and biological-context gaps remain unresolved.
