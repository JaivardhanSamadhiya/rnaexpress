# RNAddress v4 Phase B2 frozen protocol

Freeze date: 2026-09-01

Starting commit: `6ce5729fd31554e719588b8a940b0288ce0c827c`

## Scope and protected data

Only Phase B's certified Mikl/GSE173098, TDP-43/GSE288185 and Moffatt/GSE334718 cohort is eligible: 93,208 assay rows, 445 base decision sets, 890 directional tasks and 213 biological units. N-zip outcomes remain prohibited. Astrocyte outcomes, labels and sequences remain untouched. mutREL-seq and the 2026 nuclear-speckle SNVs are audit-only and excluded before B2 fitting.

Frozen representation: 3UTRBERT revision `220d80829deb077d1d640463a4267a96e9e70b1d`, checkpoint SHA-256 `7aca71823ab74771006be1030d9e7239220bba40a16858575929a02e6d2a7471`, definitive embedding-cache SHA-256 `51912767e74dc19a06dadf6bab9f3022f11939f1cc537f024af148cc18f948d9`. No encoder selection or fine-tuning is allowed.

## Splits and weights

The five Phase B biological folds are reused exactly: Mikl/TDP hold complete genes; Moffatt holds complete exact-sequence-equivalent biological parents. Every outer-test biological unit is absent from nuisance fitting, residual-target generation, context-model fitting, scaling and hyperparameter selection.

Training weights are inverse decision-set size × inverse number of sets per biological unit × inverse number of units per source, normalized to mean one. This equalizes sources, biological units and decision sets. No Moffatt parent may receive greater total source-normalized weight than another solely through row count.

## Outcomes and nuisance decomposition

Raw assay-scale `localization_effect` is retained. Nuisance features are Phase B metadata/edit geometry only: edit cost, fraction, operation size, substitution/insertion/deletion/replacement lengths, block count, position/span, nucleotide-composition changes, operation class and edit-cost tier. They contain no language-model state.

Two nuisance levels are frozen:

1. `G_global`: StandardScaler + Ridge(`alpha=100`) without source identity.
2. `G_seen_source`: the same global model plus `alpha=100` assay-context residuals; unseen target residual is exactly zero.

For every outer training fold, nuisance residual labels are generated by inner cross-fitting over the remaining biological folds. For row i, `r_i = y_i - G^{-i}(geometry_i)`. No in-sample nuisance prediction is a residual label. The outer-test baseline is predicted by a nuisance model fit only on the complete outer training set.

Primary within-source comparisons use the stronger nuisance level selected by inner regret. Leave-source-out always uses `G_global` because target-source nuisance is unavailable.

## Context features

The cached feature blocks are frozen as parent absolute 128 dimensions, mutant absolute 128 and contextual edit delta 128. B2 uses parent state and contextual delta. Fixed, outcome-independent Gaussian projections use seed `42017` and are orthonormalized where dimensions permit.

The parent-invariant null uses contextual delta plus four numeric geometry covariates and no parent state.

## Matched strata

The cross-parent nuisance stratum is:

`dataset + assay_context + intervention_class + motif_family_or_none + edit_size_band + operation_signature`.

`operation_signature` records substitution/insertion/deletion/replacement presence. Edit bands are exactly `1`, `2-5`, `6-10`, `11-25`, `26-50`, `>50`. Cross-parent strata require at least 20 rows and at least five biological units. At most two deterministic candidate rows per biological unit are retained per stratum, ordered by candidate SHA-256.

Within-parent strata use `decision_set_id + intervention_class + motif_family_or_none + edit_size_band + operation_signature`, require at least four candidates and contribute at most four deterministic pairs. Pair construction is outcome-blind; residual differences become targets only after pairing is frozen.

## Frozen model family

- `M0`: strongest cross-fitted geometry nuisance.
- `M1`: residual contextual Ridge with fixed 32-dimensional parent and 32-dimensional contextual-delta projections plus four numeric geometry covariates; `alpha ∈ {10,100,1000}` selected inside training folds.
- `M2`: M1 plus explicit elementwise parent×delta interaction at rank `r ∈ {4,8,16}`. This is a low-rank bilinear map `P(parent)^T diag(w) E(delta)`; rank and alpha are selected inside training folds.
- `M3`: M2 plus cross-parent and within-parent matched difference rows, each contrast receiving half the total weight of one ordinary row after normalization. No outcome is used to define strata or pair membership.
- `M4`: one Group-DRO-style source×direction reweighting candidate. It is eligible in an outer fold only if M2 or M3 achieves inner rank ContextValue >0 and regret ContextValue ≥0.005. Group weights are inverse group mean squared residual error from a first fit, clipped to `[0.5,2]`, followed by one refit. No iterative domain-generalization search is allowed.

M1–M4 predict residual outcome. Total score is `G + C`. Increase ranks total predicted effect descending; decrease ranks it ascending. Direction metrics are always reported separately.

## Controls

On each outer test fold, deterministic seed `42017` controls are constructed within matched nuisance strata:

- context shuffle permutes parent state while preserving delta and all geometry;
- contextual-delta shuffle permutes delta while preserving parent state and all geometry;
- interaction knockout sets only bilinear interaction features to zero;
- parent-invariant null is independently fitted without parent state.

Sparse test strata that cannot permute across at least two biological units are excluded from the corresponding control comparison and reported; they are never silently rematched using outcomes.

## Model selection

All alpha, rank, nuisance-level and model-family choices use inner biological holdouts only. Primary inner criterion is equal-source normalized-regret ContextValue; secondary criteria are rank ContextValue and GoodSelection@3 ContextValue. Ties within 0.002 regret ContextValue choose the simpler model in order M1, M2, M3, M4 and then lower rank/stronger regularization.

## Metrics

Decision-set metrics reuse Phase B definitions: directional rank percentile, normalized regret, selected experimental and normalized utility, GoodSelection@1/3/5, oracle recovery and Spearman. Evaluation macro-averages decision sets within biological unit and then biological units within source; source-level selection uses equal source weighting.

ContextValue is full minus nuisance for metrics where larger is better and nuisance minus full for regret. It is reported by biological unit, source, direction, edit band and transfer scenario. Exact 1-nt results are descriptive only.

Bootstrap uses 10,000 deterministic paired resamples of biological units with seed `42017`.

## Frozen gates

- **A — context beats geometry:** equal-source/direction regret ContextValue ≥0.010 with rank ContextValue ≥0, or rank ContextValue ≥0.020 with regret ContextValue ≥-0.005.
- **B — context necessity:** against each of context shuffle, delta shuffle and interaction knockout, full-model regret improvement ≥0.005 or rank improvement ≥0.010, with the companion metric not worse by more than 0.002.
- **C — distributed units:** median unit regret ContextValue >0; fraction improved >0.50; leave-best-unit-out mean >0; paired-bootstrap lower 95% bound ≥-0.005.
- **D — Mikl matched context:** held-gene matched-operation rank ContextValue ≥0.010 and regret ContextValue ≥0.005.
- **E — leave-source-out:** regret ContextValue ≥0.005 on at least four of six source×direction tasks and positive mean in at least two sources; no source mean below -0.010; equal-source mean ≥0.005.
- **F — cell/reporter transfer:** regret ContextValue ≥0.005 on at least two of four cell tasks and at least two of four reporter tasks.
- **G — small-edit bridge:** combined 2–10-nt rank ContextValue ≥0.010 and regret ContextValue ≥0.005; neither 2–5 nor 6–10 regret ContextValue may be below -0.005.
- **H — direction:** a direction passes only with within-source regret ContextValue ≥0.005 and rank ContextValue ≥0, leave-source-out regret ContextValue ≥0.003, small-edit regret ContextValue ≥0.003 and >50% units improved.
- **I — source robustness:** at least two source mean regret ContextValues ≥0.005 and none below -0.005.
- **J — controls/integrity:** relationship-breaking controls lose the incremental context benefit, all invariants/tests pass, N-zip is unused and Astrocyte remains sealed.

## Verdict and stop

The final verdict must be exactly one of: `GO — CONTEXTUAL DISRUPTION COMPILER`, `GO — CONTEXTUAL BIDIRECTIONAL COMPILER`, `GO — SOURCE-SPECIFIC COMPILER`, `CONDITIONAL GO — SMALL-EDIT GAP`, or `NO-GO`.

Phase B2 cannot resume DFL, open Astrocyte, generate Astrocyte predictions or build a UI. At completion, stop.
