## Classification

**NO-GO for the stated PV-Care-comparable competition target.** This is not a claim that the negative result lacks scientific value. It means the current evidence does not earn the central algorithmic or external-validation claims required by the prompt.

## Alternative-formulation check

The post-lock hostile audit does not reveal a fundamentally different high-novelty rescue supported by the same labels:

- metadata-only and GC-only controls reach descriptive rank percentiles of 0.612 and 0.594 across the 15 cross-fitted parents, close to pairwise ranking at 0.622;
- sequence-cluster-held-out pairwise ranking remains descriptive at 0.619, but does not change the untouched locked comparison;
- the strong forward model wins the locked primary comparison;
- Mikl's multi-base interventions do not produce a superior SNV recommendation model;
- an editability atlas or forward localization predictor would be scientifically useful but would weaken the requested inverse-algorithm contribution and would not satisfy the PV-Care target by itself.

Accordingly, the project stops before external reveal and software construction. A legitimate restart would require new independent internal parent-level evidence or a genuinely new preregistered method, not tuning against the sealed Astrocyte benchmark.
