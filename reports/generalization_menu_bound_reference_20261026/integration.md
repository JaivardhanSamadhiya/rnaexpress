## Attribution boundary for the three-arm study

Independent review also clarified a design limitation. On larger menus,
full-menu training and the capped 256-pair controls differ in the coverage of
candidate comparisons and potentially sampled candidates, in addition to cost
use and loss aggregation. Binary margin equivalence controls one specific
confound, not every difference.

The proposed experiment must therefore be described as a comparison of a
composite full-menu training objective against the two fixed capped-pair
objectives. A favorable result cannot be attributed uniquely to normalized
costs, full coverage, margin or temperature. No fourth arm or new fit was added
after this clarification: the three-arm plan remains 120 tasks and all actual
project-fit counts remain 0. An isolated mechanistic claim about cost weighting
would require a separately specified matched comparison.
