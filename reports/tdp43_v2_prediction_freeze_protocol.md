# TDP-43 v2 locked-prediction freeze protocol

Frozen on 2026-08-27 after the v2.6 N-zip development gate passed and before reading the TDP-43 locked outcome artifact. The 1,006-row locked feature artifact may be used; `data/frozen/outcomes/tdp43_v2_locked_outcomes.csv.gz` remains prohibited until the prediction artifact, fitted model artifacts, environment, code hashes and commit are frozen.

## Selected custom method

The selected v2.6 architecture is refit without tuning on the 3,560 permitted TDP-43 development interventions from 12 genes. Ranking groups and cross-fitting groups are `gene_id`; gene identity is never a feature. The training target is the empirical percentile of `delta_localization` within development gene.

The frozen 2,638-dimensional contextual vector is the same SpliceBERT mutant-minus-parent pooling used in v2.6 (CLS, global nucleotide, changed-nucleotide and radius-10 changed-window deltas) followed by the unchanged 590-dimensional v2 edit vector. For meta-training, the contextual Ridge prediction is generated by strict leave-one-development-gene-out cross-fitting. A final contextual Ridge is then fit on all 12 development genes and applied to the four locked genes. Standardization is training-only and Ridge alpha is fixed at 2,638.

The low-dimensional block has exactly 20 features: 18 generalized edit metadata features and the two frozen Mikl XGBoost probability deltas. The metadata mapping is 16 normalized reference-to-alternate substitution counts across all changed positions, mean changed-position fraction, and sequence length divided by 100. This exactly equals the v2.6 SNV mapping when one base is edited. The final meta Ridge receives those 20 features plus the cross-fitted contextual score, uses training-only standardization, and has fixed alpha 21.

## Frozen comparators

The strongest forward comparator is the preregistered absolute-sequence LightGBM refit on all permitted TDP-43 development parent and mutant measurements. Its prediction is `f(mutant) - f(parent)`. It uses 300 trees, learning rate 0.03, 15 leaves, minimum child samples 30, L2 penalty 1, column fraction 0.7 and seed 20260826.

The motif/accessibility comparator is a fixed 22-feature Ridge fit to the same within-gene percentile target. Its features are 12 parent-to-mutant deltas from the prespecified fixed motif/composition summaries and 10 ViennaRNA 2.7.2 structural features: changes in four global ensemble summaries, parent/mutant/delta unpaired probability at edited bases, and parent/mutant/delta unpaired probability in the radius-10 edit window. Standardization is training-only and Ridge alpha is fixed at 22. No CLIP label or gene identifier is used.

## Artifacts required before reveal

The freeze script must write, validate and hash:

- all 1,006 locked-feature rows with predictions from the custom method and both comparators;
- fitted custom contextual and meta scaler/Ridge parameters;
- the fitted LightGBM model;
- fitted motif/accessibility scaler/Ridge parameters;
- row-order, input, feature-cache, model, code and environment hashes; and
- a machine-readable manifest stating that the locked outcomes were not read.

The complete freeze is committed before any outcome access. The gate remains exactly: custom macro directional rank percentile above 0.550; positive gain over both comparators; positive within-gene Spearman for at least three of four genes; and aggregate advantage not attributable to only one locked gene. Failure leaves Astrocyte outcomes sealed.
