## Reporter processing: relevant caveat, different endpoint

[Dao et al., Nature Communications 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12297286/) documents U-rich-element-associated cryptic reporter splicing. Their reconstruction methods use GFP-through-poly-A context and SpliceAI/Pangolin; they do not establish SpliceBERT performance. External comparisons concern PTRE-seq and Griesemer/Zhao/Siegel/Fu expression/splicing assays, not our small-edit localization menus. Full reporter context matters because a local insert cannot specify a donor in an unknown upstream cassette. The article is CC BY-NC-ND4.0, while the stated author code/data license is separately MIT and the archive is DOI10.5281/zenodo.15700943. No files were admitted.

Project inference: this motivates resolving construct sequence and processing before a future donor–acceptor diagnostic. It does not show that any admitted localization label is incorrect, that all reporters splice, or that a sequence representation can correct unobserved processing. Our previous certified local DNA versus uncertain mature-RNA distinction remains. No current production input, label, split, gate or protected source is changed. A meaningful future route would require actual cassette and primer/amplicon coverage, a fixed processing hypothesis and a separate prefit design; one should not infer transcript deletion from U richness alone.

## Fresh protrusion study: compatibility unconfirmed

Its abstract describes optimized mouse non-neuronal fractionation and endogenous transcriptome-wide protrusion localization, including noncoding/pseudogene and mitochondrial categories. It specifies no designed small-edit versus WT candidate menus. That absence in the abstract is not proof that none exist; full methods and supplementary inventory would be required. The [bioRxiv page](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.17.752430v1) and PMC13596472 were inaccessible through the current web tool, and no license or files were admitted.

Project inference: endogenous localization could offer a future compatible-compartment absolute-localization audit or context prior, but absolute ranking is a different estimand from selecting edits relative to a matched WT. It cannot currently serve as a positive independent small-edit transfer test. Do not download outcome tables until metadata establishes exact sequence, species/cell/endpoint, controls, public/free terms and scientific value under a separate admission plan. No new performance-informed hypothesis is selected here.
