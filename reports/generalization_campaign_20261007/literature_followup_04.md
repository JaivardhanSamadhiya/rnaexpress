# Literature and next-route assessment - 8 October 2026
The scientific objective remains predicting measured localization effects and choosing small edits on excluded parents/genes/contexts. These sources motivate possible experiments; they do not constitute a positive project result or guarantee novelty.

## External cellular context priors
[Original Mikl methods](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/18/10643/6717835) use differentiated CAD in DMEM/F12 plus 0.8% FBS and Neuro-2a in DMEM plus 1% FBS. No independent abundance dataset pair has been certified to match both experimental states.

[CAD GSE125853](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE125853), associated with [Cherone et al.](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/29/11/1791), contains independent whole-cell WT RNA-seq across serum withdrawal. Complete withdrawal, duration, substrate and laboratory differ. [CAD GSE291553](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE291553) uses five-day serum-free differentiation with ITS in its [primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11952321/), also a different state. [N2A GSE157423](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE157423) has public total-RNA controls, but its RNA-seq culture metadata specify 10% FBS; the publication's separate 1% proteomics condition must not be reassigned to those RNA samples. These are metadata leads, with no expression matrix admitted or localization model fit.

Constant additive expression covariates cannot rank candidates within one cell/parent menu. Sequence-by-expression interactions would be required. With only two fixed signatures, cell identity, batch and expression remain confounded; RNA abundance is not protein abundance or occupancy. Such a follow-up cannot by itself identify a causal cell-state mechanism.

## Binding-resource prerequisites
The downloaded [2024 MPRNA-IP study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11162807/) measures IP/Input association in HEK293T. It supplies possible auxiliary binding supervision, not localization truth or affinity. Outcome-blind FASTA/count-ID metadata audit certified 81,343 oligos representing 3,358 named tiles. The barcode copies are technical, not independent parental genes. Source-certified WT/mutant/complement relationships, full reporter context and sample/normalization contracts are still missing for small-edit auxiliary training.

The [2026 protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13273556/) confirms the 16-nt handle, tile, 10-nt barcode and 17-nt handle architecture, but documents a different PUM2 pool. Its design does not certify ancestry of the 2024 pools. An official GSE315146 supplement inventory was preserved; no numerical data were downloaded. The Zenodo metadata request's redirect was not admitted, and that failure is preserved without automatic replacement.

## Distinct external fly panel
[Singh et al., 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41594-026-01794-8) publicly report localization comparisons involving five fly RNA families and three K10 substitution variants. The richer 2/4/6-substitution menu is concentrated in K10; the other families mostly offer deletions. Embryos are nested within injection experiments. Qualitative results are already exposed.

A bounded metadata audit should verify author sequence tables, WT/mutant junction identity and actual transcribed ends before numerical source workbooks are opened. Native accession spans differ from reported injected lengths for several constructs; supplying the bare native spans would guess assay inputs. Binding-only variants must not be admitted as localization outcomes. This panel may support a narrow external transport test, not a broad mammalian gene-held benchmark. No numerical workbook has been opened.
