# Established Pumilio biology and the remaining research claim
PUM thermodynamic affinity, energetic coupling and alternative-register integration are established by [Jarmoskaite2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6645366/). PRELib already used that occupancy script and directly compared predicted mean occupancy with binding of WT/mPRE mutants in Figure7C. It also reported NORAD tandem-site effects inadequately captured by that model. Our fixed global single-protein logZ differs from mean footprint occupancy; this difference does not establish new PUM binding physics. [Farberov-Ulitsky2024](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/52/21/13402/7848851)

General sequence-based neuronal localization prediction is also established. A potentially distinct claim would require reliable incremental small-edit selection under excluded-gene/cell settings, complete parental menus, matched controls and frozen outcomes. No such pass is demonstrated here. A finite literature search is not proof of complete novelty. [Mikl2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9561380/)

The immediate pooled-binding study is auxiliary methodological validation on an exposed development resource. Passing its demanding declared criterion would justify testing a prior; it could not become the project's localization result or independent confirmation. Reproduced nulls, assay/context contradictions and code-convention differences must remain visible. Neither positivity nor an STS selection outcome can be guaranteed.
