# Generalization campaign: what each test can establish

7 October 2026. Public, free, local computational work only. This campaign is an exposed-data development effort. Neither success on an individual comparison nor a favourable confidence interval constitutes independent prospective validation.

## Objective and current evidence

The objective is to select useful small RNA sequence edits in biological contexts excluded from model fitting and selection. A transferable candidate ordering is a stronger claim than prediction of intact-transcript localization, training-set correlation, or a favourable computationally generated sequence.

Independent arithmetic checks reproduced all 26,258 admitted contrasts. Original author files separately supported all 24,514 Mikl, Moffatt and astrocyte rows to numerical precision. Those findings resolve important sequence/label join questions; they do not certify every raw-count pipeline, RNA species, construct boundary or causal attribution.

The first five-route campaign failed its fixed generalization gate. A subsequent fixed endpoint-orientation comparison improved mean regret from 0.51225485 to 0.47330564, with shared-component bootstrap improvement interval [0.02205287, 0.05713873]. It also failed its gate, including the absolute-regret and avoidable-harm requirements. This is evidence motivating a declared alignment comparison, not an achieved reliable edit predictor. All prior NO-GO decisions remain intact.

## Complementary routes and their distinct questions

| Route | Question | Required controls and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Structure ensemble | Do predicted accessibility and pairing changes add transferable information? | Same sequence counts without accessibility; fixed ViennaRNA specification; exact cache integrity and independent fresh folds. Predictions are not measured structure. |
| Author 3UTRBERT | Does an author-certified pretrained contextual representation improve edit ordering? | Identical projection applied to a noncontextual token lookup, plus the base features. Public pretraining is not gene-disjoint biological confirmation. |
| Structure plus encoder | Are the two information sources complementary? | Must improve over both individual routes, under the same gate. |
| RBP motif specificity and accessibility | Does experimentally derived RBP sequence specificity, then predicted accessibility, improve ordering? | Raw motifs against base; accessible motifs against raw. Scores are specificity proxies, not occupancy, affinity or causal RBP activity. No licensed original catalog redistribution. |
| Fixed endpoint alignment factorial | Does the known measurement direction obscure transferable sequence effects? | Each aligned track must improve over its identical unaligned feature track. Endpoint signs are fixed from metadata; target labels cannot select signs. Export and distal transport remain distinct biology. |
| Crossed gene and cell transfer | Does a compatible localization endpoint transfer to both unseen genes and an unseen cell? | Both cell directions, target gene/component and exact allele exclusion, source-only inner selection, simple and base controls. A narrower claim than universality across four heterogeneous assays. |
| SpliceBERT | Does a different pretrained representation of processing-related sequence context help? | Exact native/OpenVINO parity before project inference; single-base lookup control; same strict selection gate. The 46-nt SRLE context is outside the author's recommended training lengths. |

Extraction caches are shared where specifications match. Feature production precedes a separate committed prefit freeze. Source-only inner folds choose one of three predeclared regularization values. No target outcome chooses features, signs, hyperparameters, rows, gates or follow-up reporting thresholds within a frozen generation. Observed results can motivate a separately declared future generation, which remains development.

## Audits that can explain failure without manufacturing a positive result

The reporter audit certifies conditional local DNA construction and reference junctions, not complete mature RNA. Mikl WT/mutant primer cores and barcodes differ beyond the 150-nt biological insert; their common additive offsets can cancel in within-parent ranking, while candidate-specific barcode and nonlinear context effects can remain. SRLE's six-mer bridges HBB and vector sequence; substituting the entire natural HBB 3′ UTR would add un-cloned sequence. Full-context features require further construct and RNA-processing provenance.

An independent exact-menu context-conflict audit is being developed. Its finite observed-data optimum is a descriptive bound under a declared shared-utility objective; it is neither a Bayes error estimate nor a noise ceiling. Differing menus must be excluded or analysed under a separately declared question.

## Interpretation and remaining decisive evidence

A positive result must pass the entire declared gate and its information controls, survive independent reconstruction of source-only selection and held-out predictions, and retain its biological scope. Failure means that specified route did not meet that test. It does not prove the biological mechanism is absent, all RNA models are ineffective, or generalization is impossible.

The largest remaining gap is independent, compatible candidate-edit outcomes with certified assay and construct context. Repeated analysis of these four exposed studies cannot supply that independence. Reserved outcomes remain closed. If a development route passes, the next step is to specify the final model and independently evaluate an admissible untouched outcome set under its existing restrictions, rather than continue selecting on that set.

Novelty must be assessed against existing localization models, context-aware reporter models, and representation-guided RNA editing. A defensible contribution would require a specific validated advance, such as gene-and-cell-excluded candidate ordering with demonstrable incremental information, rather than the broad claim that sequence, structure, RBP motifs or foundation models can affect RNA localization.
