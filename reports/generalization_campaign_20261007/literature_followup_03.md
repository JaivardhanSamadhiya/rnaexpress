# Primary-literature follow-up and current interpretation

This is subsequent observed development, not independent confirmation. It preserves all earlier protocols, gates and failed results. New computational feasibility is not a positive localization result.

## Why the remaining routes are distinct

1. **Direct RBP sequence preferences:** experimental position-frequency matrices add protein-specific sequence priors. The raw versus accessibility-weighted control tests whether modeled accessibility adds information beyond sequence preferences. These scores do not measure cellular affinity, occupancy or protein activity. The 2025 [motif resource](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02733-6) is prior art for the motif representation; the potential contribution here would be demonstrated candidate-edit transfer.
2. **Joint site opening:** all bases in a binding window being unpaired is different from their average unpaired marginal. The separately admitted constrained partition calculation tests this known accessibility hypothesis against the original marginal representation and a duplicated-column regularization control. It does not establish in-cell folding or RBP causality. See [RNA accessibility and binding prediction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2874161/).
3. **Nonlinear versus matched pointwise regression:** the crossed-cell experiment compares the same fourteen fixed feature/control tracks under matched source-only losses, splits and weighting. A gain cannot automatically be attributed to nonlinearity because the earlier pairwise model also differs in loss and readout. Represented-cell evaluation will reuse the same fitted models and is a distinct generalization axis.
4. **GQ folding potential:** [neuronal GQ localization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3128965/) and [FMRP localization evidence](https://elifesciences.org/articles/52621) motivate a narrow folding-feature comparison. The isolated simplified ensemble is not a cellular GQ occupancy assay; [in-cell unfolding](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5367264/) limits its interpretation. The five fixed tracks and sequence/ordinary-structure controls were reviewed and frozen before project folding.
5. **Masked nucleotide preferences:** token logits provide a different readout from pooled hidden embeddings. The new probe compares the verified author MLM head and accepted encoder against a strict native reference. Shared union masks produce a block-conditional marginal surrogate, not a joint mutation likelihood, full pseudo-log-likelihood, evolutionary fitness or localization probability. The 2026 [zero-shot RNA-model benchmark](https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/27/2/bbag098/8509095) reports task and RNA-class dependence; it does not validate this project's localization use case. Positive and negative copies of one scalar would be redundant under symmetric linear fitting, so they are not separate hypotheses.
6. **Coordinate-certified evolutionary information:** exact author-reference and genome/exon mapping must precede any score query. Native-site constraint can distinguish edited positions but cannot distinguish alternate bases at the same position by itself. Alternate-consensus features require aligned bases, explicit strand/reference certification and an independently declared missingness rule. A parent-only average cancels within a candidate menu. Missing mappings are not zero conservation.

## New structural evidence and its practical limit

The May 2026 [Egl–BicD study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41594-026-01794-8) provides structural evidence in Drosophila for recognition involving bent stem loops, positioned base pairs and coincident RNA elements. This supports investigating whether coarse marginal summaries omit relevant arrangement information. It does not establish the same recognition mechanism in the mammalian assays or permit substituting two-dimensional predicted structure for a measured three-dimensional bend. Current ordinary caches retain unpaired marginals, pairing entropy, expected partner distance and ensemble energy; they do not retain complete pairing-partner matrices. Those summaries cannot reconstruct the lost pair identities or three-dimensional shape. No numerical source-data outcomes from this paper were downloaded for a new test.

## What the completed development results actually support

All five completed linear stream gates remain **NO-GO**. The fixed 3UTRBERT whole-assay track reduced macro regret to 0.468185, with a 0.028683 gain over the frozen best simple controls, but its benefit was concentrated in Astrocyte and it harmed Mikl. The endpoint-aligned raw-context control reached 0.465836, improving its unaligned counterpart by 0.034491, but failed the required breadth/protection checks; Moffatt avoidable wrong-direction harm reached 0.083333. Neither result supports broad generalization. Structure and embeddings did not improve the completed crossed-cell or represented-cell gates. The fixed similarity exclusion was an edit-distance definition, not a biological homology certificate.

The numerical audits independently reproduced the completed outputs; they did not find a concrete arithmetic error that rescues those failures. They also did not certify raw biological measurements, experimental noise ceilings, complete mature reporters, unsupervised pretraining disjointness or a truly independent confirmation set. Resource failures remain computational incidents, not negative biology.
