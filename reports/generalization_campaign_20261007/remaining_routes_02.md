# Remaining routes: second bounded primary-source review

7 October 2026. This review used primary papers, official documentation and historical source/configuration text. No new dataset, weight or outcome file was downloaded, no reserved outcome was opened, no model or numerical feature matrix was read, and no experiment was fitted. Existing verdicts and freezes remain unchanged. These are established biological/computational priors, not claims of a new mechanism or guaranteed generalization.

1. **Site-specific evolutionary conservation and consensus-structure disruption.** This adds information external to the assayed sequence, unlike the current near-sequence purge (a split robustness control) or encoders trained on sequence. [Taliaferro et al., 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4798900/) found conserved regulatory sequences and more conserved secondary structure in neurite-localized alternative last exons, using homologous mouse/human/rat/dog/cow UTRs and RNAalifold. That is isoform/localization evidence, not demonstrated small-edit ranking. The [official UCSC mm10 conservation track](https://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgTrackUi?db=mm10&g=cons60way) supplies Multiz, phyloP and phastCons; [regional access](https://genome.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/help/api.html) avoids whole-genome downloads, and [UCSC terms](https://genome.ucsc.edu/license/) permit public-data/API use subject to track-specific restrictions and attribution. No evolutionary conservation/covariation feature was found in the searched Python source; a binding-delta 'conservation invariant' is algebraic bookkeeping, not phylogenetic conservation. Before admission, every natural parent must be uniquely mapped to a pinned assembly, strand, transcript/exonic coordinates and exact reference sequence. A parent-average score alone cancels within-parent rankings: features must concern the edited positions and alternate alleles, such as conserved-base/consensus disruption. Missing or ambiguous mappings must remain explicit. Synthetic SRLE inserts have no equivalent native genomic annotation. Conservation can reflect many UTR functions and may predict effect magnitude rather than localization direction. Fixed conservation-only, sequence/structure-matched and position-shifted annotation controls would make this falsifiable. Coverage certification is the next step; neither eligibility nor computational time has been measured.

2. **Explicit IGF2BP1/ZBP1 two-piece binding geometry.** [Chao et al., 2010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2807350/) and [Patel et al., 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3258965/) establish two recognition pieces, their spacing, both linear orders and a localization example. Patel's Bioinformatics specification is `CGGAC-(N10–25)-[AC]CA[CU]` OR the reverse order; N counts strictly intervening RNA nucleotides. A compact exact paired-site delta can test a protein-specific spatial prior. Optional later joint opening would constrain the two recognized pieces while leaving their spacer unrestricted; that differs from making one entire contiguous PFM interval unpaired. This is partially overlapping prior art in the project: [Mechanism-v3's dictionary](D:/rnaexpress/configs/mechanism_v3/grammar_design.json) already contains the isolated `ACACCC` beta-actin proxy, while its scorer adds independent motif counts; no explicit CGGAC-plus-second-piece geometry was found in source/config searches. Encoders/RBPNet may represent it implicitly. Therefore the test needs matched isolated-piece and old-proxy controls, all original candidate menus, and an outcome-blind support audit before any fitting. Binding geometry is not measured occupancy, IGF2BP1 protein activity or a universal localization mechanism. Nuclear retention and operational astrocyte fractions are not established domains for this prior. The exact published rule is cheap to scan with the standard library; whether enough independent parents/genes actually change paired sites is unknown. A separate feasibility namespace is being prepared.

3. **G-quadruplex/ordinary-stem competition.** This is a pending earlier proposal, not a new discovery in this search. [Subramanian et al., 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3128965/) and [Goering et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32510328/) provide neuronal localization evidence. Existing G-rich/G-run summaries do not themselves calculate competition with stems. The [official ViennaRNA tutorial](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViennaRNA/ViennaRNA/master/doc/source/tutorial/RNAfold.rst) documents G-quadruplex-aware equilibrium folding, with a simplified layer/total-linker-length model that omits linker identity/asymmetry and strand orientation. A future compact mutation delta could compare G-quadruplex-on/off ensemble energy and correctly certified structured-domain probabilities, controlling for ordinary folds and raw G-rich patterns. The ordinary base-pair matrix is not a quadruplex-occupancy certificate. Existing local reporter-window uncertainty persists. Importantly, [Guo and Bartel, 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5367264/) found potential RNA quadruplexes overwhelmingly unfolded in the tested eukaryotic cells. Thus any gain would support folding-potential prediction, not prove folded quadruplex-mediated transport. Synthetic API/probability validation and runtime benchmarking remain prerequisites.

## Duplicates and unsuitable independent tests

* **Cell-conditioned joint learning is already attempted.** `src/modeling/v4_decision_models.py:76–77` defines dataset/assay/reporter/cell context; `:142–175` implements shared global predictions plus per-context ridge residuals. `src/analysis/run_v4_phaseB_models.py:186–202` pools rows outside a biological gene fold, without excluding either Mikl cell, and evaluates both global and hierarchical scores. Its target (`v4_decision_models.py:107–115`) is signed within-menu min-max utility, so this was candidate selection, not only absolute localization prediction. FinalShot M2 likewise uses raw binding summaries plus expression-by-binding interactions (`src/modeling/finalshot_models.py:125–138`) in both-cell pooled gene-fold training (`src/analysis/run_finalshot_direct_models.py:158–161`), targeting within-menu percentile ranks (`:55–65`). For two unequal expression constants, raw-plus-expression interactions have the same unregularized function class as two independent cell coefficients; the penalty couples them and does not imply identical fitted models. FinalShot M3 positive monotone assay/cell heads alone cannot change within-cell ranking. New shared+cell deviation models using current features/purges/loss would be a controlled reassessment, not a missing biological idea or unseen-cell validation.
* **SEERS/TALE is relevant prior art, not a new compatible outcome stream by default.** The [primary author preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.09.658412v2.full) already models contextual short-element nuclear/cytoplasmic behavior and prioritizes variant effects. Its A549/HCT116 random-insert design and expression-coupled nuclear/cytoplasmic endpoints do not automatically supply declared parent–small-substitution menus or neuronal projection effects. Parent lineage, replicate pairing, exact constructs, eligibility, licensing and leakage would need independent metadata admission before using outcomes. No such admission was performed here.
* **EukaUTR is a future representation audit, not a certified new validation source.** The [author repository](https://github.com/meilanglang/EukaUTR) describes species-disjoint Stage-1 training and GC-linked embedding structure, but labels its citation 'Manuscript'. Code MIT licensing does not certify checkpoint/data licensing; exclusion of exact target alleles/homologs, downloadable provenance and CPU feasibility remain unverified. It cannot replace a compatible independent intervention test.
* **Protected/unadmitted Wen/N-zip/TDP sources stay closed.** Different nuclear compartments, endogenous atlases, whole-UTR/isoform changes and small low-breadth motif-deletion studies cannot be relabeled as broad small-substitution confirmation. This bounded search found no newly certified independent compatible candidate-menu study. That is not proof that none exists.

Prioritize exact metadata feasibility for the two-piece zipcode rule and evolutionary coordinates. Keep G-quadruplex competition as a separately validated lower-priority backend probe. Any subsequent evaluation is another exposed-development experiment unless independent compatible truth is admitted under a separate prospective protocol.
