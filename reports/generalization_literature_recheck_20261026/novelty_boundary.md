This bounded primary-source recheck was performed while the fixed replay ran,
without inspecting new outer performance, changing the study or admitting new
datasets. It does not certify exhaustive novelty.

The original [Mikl et al. study](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/18/10643/6717835)
already reports machine-learning prediction of reporter localization and
comparison with endogenous transcripts. It discusses sequence context and the
limitations of two neuronal cell lines and short reporter tiles. The
[GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE173098)
confirms that this is the existing CAD/Neuro-2a reporter resource. Reusing that
resource therefore cannot by itself establish a new biological context.

[Evgeniou and Pontil's original working paper](https://flora.insead.edu/fichiersti_wp/inseadwp2004/2004-11.pdf)
describes regularized task learning with a shared parameter vector and
task-specific deviations. Thus shared-plus-cell-specific shrinkage has
established methodological prior art; the current pairwise RNA application
must be assessed for its specific contribution.

Our inference is that any defensible new claim must rest on the precise edit
decision task, exclusion units, competitive controls and distributed benefit,
followed by the stated biological scope. A passing current gate would concern
held genes in represented cell lines. It would not establish unseen-cell
transfer, a causal RNA-binding mechanism, or a first-ever localization model.
A negative gate cannot be rescued by calling a secondary metric the primary.

The exact relationship to every original paper split/model comparison remains
unresolved: direct full-method access was partly blocked by PMC/OUP tool
responses. Indexed primary text and the original multitask paper were used;
blocked URLs/tokens were not retained. No raw measurements or supplementary
outcome tables were downloaded. The precise candidate-edit protocol may still
differ from prior work, but absence from this short search is not proof of
novelty. This recheck is separate from the pending numerical/gate verdict.
