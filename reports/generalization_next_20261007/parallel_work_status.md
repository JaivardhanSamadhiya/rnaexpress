The research objective is to rank small candidate edits for localization effects in biological contexts excluded from training. A favorable transcript-localization classifier score, known-parent repeat transfer, or one exposed reporter does not establish this objective. Computational work uses local hardware, public free resources and no paid service. Protected confirmation outcomes remain closed.

These are descriptive exposed-data observations, not a calibrated noise ceiling or proof of a particular RBP mechanism.

## Confidence gaps

The admitted endpoints are not interchangeable physical quantities. Several studies contain very few independent genes/reporters. The SRLE full biological production chain and Moffatt paired replicate contrast data remain incomplete. Ensemble accessibility is a marginal-probability proxy, not joint opening or occupancy. Catalog preferences and RNA abundance proxies are not active cellular RBP concentrations. Public sequence pretraining may include natural parent sequences, so outcome exclusion does not establish unseen pretraining sequence history. Existing results and every new comparison here are exposed-data development evidence. Literature review cannot certify exhaustive novelty or guarantee a competition result.
