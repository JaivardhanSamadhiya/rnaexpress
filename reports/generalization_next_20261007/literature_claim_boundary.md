# Literature findings and what this experiment can establish

The literature supports testing contextual sequence representations, RNA accessibility and RBP preferences, while leaving the project's candidate-edit transfer question unresolved. [3UTRBERT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11497048/) already predicts absolute localization classes. [DeepLocRNA](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/40/2/btae065/7601323) already uses binding-related pretraining for localization; its methods also select classification thresholds on test data. Neither result establishes the present whole-assay small-edit regret objective. A positive result cannot be described merely as the first use of RNA embeddings or RBP information for localization.

Mechanism papers motivate specific hypotheses, rather than universal signs for every assay. [CNBP–KIF1C transport](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39982819/) supports GA-rich cargo recognition in tested protrusion systems. [Position-dependent localization across cell morphologies](https://elifesciences.org/articles/80040) shows why a motif's transcript region matters. The new RBP catalog covers broader experimental preferences, but matching a motif does not measure active cellular protein or establish causality.

Full transcript production and processing may matter beyond a local sequence. An [HBB reporter study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1097276524005938) reports different export competence for intron-containing and intronless precursors despite identical mature RNAs. This grounds an unresolved processing confound; it does not prove that cryptic splicing causes the admitted SRLE effects. The verified46nt construction context improves input provenance while leaving the complete expressed reporter unresolved.

The parallel experiments retain exact development cohorts, source-only model selection and unchanged outcome gates. They test incremental information against fixed controls. They cannot certify entirely new biological sequences after public sequence pretraining, complete mechanism, calibrated absolute benefit, independent biological confirmation, exhaustive novelty or a competition outcome. Preserve all positive and negative route results and retain the need for a separately designed compatible confirmation.

The cell-context review also found a previously attempted expression-conditioned RBPNet route, which failed its original gate. Reusing the same expression files is a reassessment, not an untouched hypothesis. The separate single-base SpliceBERT preparation represents another pretraining prior, with an explicit short-input mismatch for SRLE and a resource admission check before conversion.
