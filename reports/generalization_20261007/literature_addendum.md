# Additional literature and resource check, 7 October 2026

The primary SRLE paper explicitly places randomized six-mers into the HBB 3′UTR and distinguishes intron-containing and intronless reporters. Its methods state that oligo homology arms are in Table S1. These construct details matter for a future complete-context encoder or folding experiment; a generic HBB reference transcript is not enough to certify the reporter's mature sequence and insertion boundary. No new biological outcome was used here. [Primary SRLE methods](https://spj.science.org/doi/10.34133/csbj.0107).

The Moffatt source is a 2026 preprint, not a peer-reviewed result. Its authors report approximately 200-nt minimal active elements with distinct sequence-order and composition requirements. This supports testing explicit ordered/context representations but does not establish that any new predictor will succeed. [Author preprint record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42327238/).

mRNABERT is an additional publicly accessible pretrained resource. The official model card lists Apache-2.0 and a 456-MB checkpoint; its code preprocesses UTRs with individual bases and CDS with codons. This makes exact region annotation and checkpoint/code review prerequisites. A coding-aware pretrained model is not automatically a better small-3′UTR-edit predictor; inference cost, biological task match, source overlap and mutation sensitivity must be measured before a new supervised experiment. The existing local 3UTRBERT checkpoint is the more efficient initial encoder candidate. No mRNABERT download, paid inference or installation was needed for this generation. [Primary mRNABERT paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65340-8), [official code](https://github.com/yyly6/mRNABERT), [official checkpoint/license inventory](https://huggingface.co/YYLY66/mRNABERT/tree/main).

The group-DRO method motivates increased attention to worst-source risk, with appropriate regularization. It addresses worst-group training distributions and does not guarantee a shared rule under a new biological endpoint. DomainBed further emphasizes that held-domain model selection must be specified; the current experiment therefore uses source-only assay holdouts rather than choosing penalties on the target assay. [Original group-DRO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731), [DomainBed primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01434).
