## Errors and overly strong historical conclusions

1. **The earliest failures have a documented label-integrity defect.** The
   August 29 N-zip incident records 491 workbook entries with exactly zero ratio
   and missing adjusted P value. Historical processing treated that unresolved
   state as a measured zero. Of 4,395 retained SNVs, 951 involved an unresolved
   mutant or WT value. The attempted independent historical truth reconstruction
   later failed its coverage and numerical-reproduction requirements. Therefore
   original v1/v2/v3 ranking, magnitude, confidence and control results remain
   historical failures on an uncertified target; they are not clean evidence
   that the intended biology cannot generalize. Sequence identities/features are
   separable from that label defect. TDP localization truth itself was not thereby
   invalidated, but the tested old systems inherited N-zip training/selection.
   Sources: `v3_5_historical_outcome_integrity_incident.md`,
   `v3_5r_historical_impact_matrix.md`, `v3_5r_truth_reconstruction_verdict.md`.

2. **Fitted leaky models are not an upper bound on achievable performance.**
   Mechanism-v3 calls particular sibling-mutant models a measured ceiling and
   concludes that no method can pass. A finite list of fitted linear/nonlinear
   models is neither the Bayes predictor nor an oracle bound. The reliability
   estimate of 0.239 depends on the uncertainty estimator, target construction
   and assumptions; it does not prove 76% irreducible noise for every selection
   task. Later direct replicate-selection diagnostics also show useful extreme
   selection despite low all-pair agreement. Preserve the actual v3 failures and
   instability; withdraw the impossibility inference.

3. **Random projection of informative k-mers is not a biological null.**
   Mechanism-v5 claims that comparable random-projection performance proves no
   higher-order grammar recoverable at any capacity. A projection of the same
   informative sequence space can preserve much or all relevant information and
   change regularization; equal performance does not imply absence of grammar.
   Mechanism-v2's random-kmer comparator does challenge unique value from the
   mechanistic stack, but does not erase sequence-order information. Intervening
   on labels or feature/allele relationships poses a different null question.

4. **Repeated analyses are not independent biological reproductions.**
   The phrase "third independent reproduction" in v5 refers to reanalyses of
   overlapping, exposed sources with different methods/tasks. They can reveal
   methodological consistency but are not three new biological validation
   experiments. The same applies to thousands of SRLE anchors, composition
   groups, overlapping swaps and constituent replicate checks.

5. **Strong simple baselines do not prove the entirety of biological signal is
   one scalar.** AU-only success and a small increment from a tested k-mer model
   support a strong baseline and failure of the declared incremental criterion.
   They do not establish that all remaining sequence/context information is
   absent, that capacity can never matter, or that one tested source's AU sign is
   a universal mechanistic sign. The v4 statement assigning roughly 93% of
   achievable discrimination to composition has no additive AUC decomposition.
   Comparing its 0.95 AUC with another publication's 0.81–0.83 on a different
   cohort/task is not a matched improvement test.

6. **Resource failures and provenance failures differ from biological model
   failures.** Excluded Parnet artifacts did not match the audited paper model;
   unadmitted stability, mutREL or Wen resources were not valid tests of those
   mechanisms. HeLa/SEERS failure reports retain unresolved measurement mapping,
   depth or independence. Their numerical failures should not be presented as
   clean mechanistic disproof. The later strict SRLE result is a simple,
   prespecified control result; the positional-pair primary failed its strict
   magnitude comparison. Do not retroactively promote the control to a passed
   primary.

The old experiment-specific NO-GO verdicts remain preserved. The corrections
above narrow their interpretation; they neither manufacture a GO nor forbid a
new explicitly authorized, separately frozen investigation of different
questions.

## Important later results beyond that ledger

**Strict-purge SRLE:** the simple 2-mer control retained 10.19% edit-change MSE
improvement with a descriptive interval 1.00–19.99%, while the original
positional-pair primary failed strict magnitude prediction. Additive 1–3-mers
selected candidates at regret about 0.320 versus exact uniform 0.500, with 26.7%
wrong-both choices in constituent repeats. Entire composition groups and nearby
training alleles were excluded, strengthening sequence-level generalization
within that one HBB reporter. It does not provide a new biological context,
full source-production reconstruction, or independent mechanism validation.

**September 26 astrocyte transfer:** exact SRLE coefficients produced weak
uncertain rank correlation, below composition. An independently frozen
astrocyte representation-replication study gave candidate regret 0.372 versus
simple 0.489, but failed its primary incremental-order criterion: the paired
rank increment was 0.013, interval −0.022 to 0.068. Five nonoverlap components
nest inside only two genes; later gene-level grouping is stricter. The positive
candidate secondary result and failed primary must both remain visible.

**September 27 four-assay ranker:** best descriptive interaction_3 macro regret
was 0.4882, with clear assay-dependent benefit/harm and no passing model.
The archived BERT-plus-kmer comparison improved matched Mikl/Moffatt regret
in both directions of study holdout, but had only two-study coverage and
inherited mixed-runtime provenance. This is a specific representational lead,
not four-system validation. The inspected primary loss, direction reversal,
weight hierarchy and purge guard are internally coherent. Candidate-SD scaling,
fixed penalty, sparse pair coverage and short ordered context remain testable
limitations; see `independent_audit.md`.

**September 28 failure diagnostic:** measured other-replicate selection beat
uniform even when individual replicate order agreement was weak. The diagnostic
did not establish a formal ceiling. Exact feature collisions were rare, which
does not imply linear capacity or adequate biological encoding. Parent-constant
features initially inflated marginal out-of-distribution flags; the correction
properly focused on ranking-active features. Joint distribution shift remains
unidentified.

**September 28 probabilistic supervision:** P1/P2/P3 changed macro regret from
H0 0.4882 to 0.5031/0.5057/0.4894. All primary gates failed. P2's probability
scores improved over H0, but primary models remained worse than constant 0.5
against held-out empirical replicate support. This narrows the tested
supervision hypothesis under one representation and penalty; it does not rule
out all uncertainty models, explain the biology uniquely, or prove that raw
replicate-win and latent-mean-sign probabilities are interchangeable estimands.

## What should guide a new experiment

The credible positives are bounded and useful: within-reporter SRLE sequence
order, source-specific across-gene edit-direction prediction, known-parent
absolute localization, some extreme candidate selection, and fixed simple
policies in a repeated Faraway collection. Several primary negative results are
also informative: complex methods have often failed strong simple controls,
outcome-specific transfer, distributed benefit or harm limits.

The remaining gap is not just achieving a favorable average. A transferable
candidate selector must improve decisions under explicit endpoint/context
definitions, retain competitive controls, avoid a single component driving the
gain, and demonstrate that trustworthy inputs/measurements support the claim.
Data integrity, ordered context, loss/regularization and source balancing can
be tested separately. New routes must not alter or reinterpret old freezes,
promote old controls to primaries, open reserved tests to rescue results, or call
reused data independent confirmation. The 7 October routes are fresh controlled
development investigations under the user's new authorization; they are not a
guarantee of generalization, mechanistic novelty, or a competition result.
