# Literature-informed routes to generalization

Prepared 2026-10-07. This memo reviews primary papers and official software sources; it does not establish a positive result. Previous experiment outputs are historical evidence subject to an independent methods audit. All proposed comparisons require frozen specifications and selection using training sources only. The four currently exposed assays support development and stress testing; repeating experiments on their outcomes does not create a new prospective confirmation set.

## Biological interpretation that changes the model design

The admitted target is a mutant-minus-parent localization contrast. A neuron neurite/soma ratio, astrocyte synaptoneurosome/input ratio, and nuclear/cytoplasmic ratio are different observable endpoints. Reorienting every ratio toward its named destination does not establish that one sequence rule has the same sign or magnitude in all of them. A conditional shared mechanism is a plausible hypothesis; an unconditional universal localization utility is a stronger hypothesis.

Direct experiments show that GA-rich 3′UTR elements and pyrimidine-rich regulatory elements can act across cell morphologies. The same study found that efficient PRRE activity requires 5′UTR placement, and identified differences in La-family RBP expression between cellular contexts. These observations motivate endpoint/context-aware models while warning against applying a published motif outside its tested transcript region. [Goering et al., eLife 2023](https://elifesciences.org/articles/80040).

Nuclear retention is also contextual. HNRNPK-associated C-rich elements can promote nuclear accumulation, but binding alone is insufficient: the positions and surrounding sequence of the SIRLOIN element matter. [Lubelsky and Ulitsky, Nature 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature25757), [Lubelsky et al., EMBO Journal 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8204871/). Separately, a study of linear and circular reporter RNAs reports context-specific localization effects, supporting the need to test rather than assume transfer. [Ron and Ulitsky, Nature Communications 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30183-0).

## Route 1 — Mechanism-oriented sequence architecture

**Priority: immediate, CPU-feasible.** Preserve the existing 246 features and append a small, fixed block capturing the edit's changes to purine-rich spatial clustering and C-rich core arrangement. The baseline already contains 1–3-mer deltas and local parent-composition interactions. It does not explicitly encode a long purine run, a concentration of purines within longer windows, an intact 5–7-base C-rich core, or arrangements of multiple cores.

The implemented block in `src/generalization_20261007/route_mechanism.py` has 125 columns: 25 mutant-minus-parent summaries and their interactions with four symmetric parent/mutant sequence-context summaries. It uses fixed 8/16/32/64-nt windows touching edited sites, whole-insert purine-run summaries, overlapping CCTCC/CCTCCC/[AG]CCTCCC counts, 29/42/54-nt core architecture windows, and two fixed core-spacing kernels. Windows and kernels are prespecified computational hypotheses, not experimentally established optimal cutoffs. Every input is exact sequence. Counts use available sequence lengths and motif starts; no padding or outcome-dependent normalization occurs. One feature block and three L2 penalties (.005/.05/.5) are permitted.

The GA-rich rationale comes from experiments identifying A/G-rich localization elements and showing that longer sequence context contributes to their activity. A/G richness alone was insufficient to predict endogenous RNA localization. [Arora et al., Nucleic Acids Research 2022](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/18/10626/6701598). A KIF1C–CNBP complex provides an independent molecular connection between GA-rich 3′UTRs and peripheral transport. This does not imply that CNBP mediates every admitted reporter assay. [Moissoglu et al., Cell Reports 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12002053/). Recent systematic mutagenesis also supports multipartite, relatively long localization elements. [Moffatt et al., bioRxiv 2026 preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42327238/).

**Assumption:** these explicit compositional/arrangement changes retain useful effects across parents and assays after source-only scaling. **Falsification:** no improvement over the matched 246-feature model under held-assay evaluation; effect disappears after composition-matched arrangement controls; gains are confined to one parent/study. Reversing an edit must negate the additional features, identical sequences must yield exact zero, and feature construction must be independent of outcome columns. Eight synthetic scoped tests verify the implementation semantics. PRRE/5′TOP features are excluded because the admitted 3′UTR/core sequences do not establish appropriate 5′UTR context.

## Route 2 — Exact ordered mutation representation

**Priority: immediate, CPU-feasible in parallel.** Append sparse exact 4–6-mer mutant-minus-parent counts to the original features. This represents intact short sequence motifs and their disruption more directly than 1–3-mers. It is distinct from route 1: it learns ordered sequence effects without choosing a localization mechanism or RBP identity. Longer context is supported by the SIRLOIN and neuronal mutagenesis studies cited above, but the literature does not prove that these particular k-mer lengths will generalize.

**Assumption:** transferable local sequence specificity was compressed away by the original representation. **Falsification:** failure against the same baseline under source-only selection, no benefit on composition-preserving substitutions, or comparable performance after within-parent shuffled labels. Include matched feature scaling, unsupported-column zero coefficients, exact affected-start/full-count equivalence, and gene/any-allele exclusion across folds. More dimensions alone are not evidence of biological mechanism.

## Route 3 — Sequence structure and accessibility perturbation

**Priority: subsequent independent branch if runtime already available or free installation is practical.** Compute mutant-minus-parent ensemble pairing/accessibility around changed sites on exact admitted inserts; include motif accessibility interactions. Prefer ensemble quantities over a single minimum-energy fold. Use the same insert boundaries for parent and mutant. The original 246 features do not model base pairing, but reporter insert folding does not necessarily equal full reporter/transcript folding.

The primary RNA-binding affinity landscape study measured sequence, structure, and context preferences across 78 human RBPs. [Dominguez et al., Molecular Cell 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6062212/). ViennaRNA provides partition functions, local pairing probabilities, and accessibility functions, with free research/educational/commercial use subject to attribution and redistribution terms. [Official repository](https://github.com/ViennaRNA/ViennaRNA), [exact copyright terms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ViennaRNA/ViennaRNA/master/COPYING).

**Assumption:** changes in accessibility or long-range pairing supply transferable information. **Falsification:** no gain beyond matched composition features, instability to prespecified boundary/context sensitivity analyses, or apparent benefit reproduced by GC/length-only controls. Global folding energy is not enough: the astrocyte study reports no overall localization association with global secondary-structure/GC features. [Official Astrocyte study GEO metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE330741). Freeze temperature, span/window, feature aggregation, and boundary sensitivity before outcome comparisons.

## Route 4 — Frozen pretrained representations of mutation effects

**Priority: reuse local free caches first; larger downloads/inference last.** Evaluate frozen parent/mutant embedding differences, with a fixed changed-site pooling rule and a small source-trained ranking head. Full-sequence averaging can dilute a one-base effect; retaining the changed-site representation is a separate, explicit hypothesis. Audit tokenizer normalization, truncation, checkpoint hashes, model training corpus, and any overlap with evaluation sequences.

RNA-FM is a self-supervised RNA encoder with publicly available code/checkpoint links and an MIT software license. Its official documentation distinguishes ncRNA-trained RNA-FM from a CDS-trained mRNA-FM; neither training scope automatically establishes fitness for mammalian 3′UTR small edits. [Official RNA-FM repository](https://github.com/ml4bio/RNA-FM), [MIT license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ml4bio/RNA-FM/main/LICENSE), [primary RNA-FM paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00300).

RNALoc-LM provides a localization-oriented downstream model but its released tasks are lncRNA, circRNA, and miRNA classification. It is evidence that RNA-FM embeddings can support localization classification, not that an mRNA point-edit ranker transfers. Its repository is public and links trained models; no explicit repository software license was observed during this review. [Primary RNALoc-LM paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/41/4/btaf127/8090438), [official code/task inventory](https://github.com/CSUBioGroup/RNALoc-LM).

RNA-GPS uses separate 5′UTR/CDS/3′UTR inputs and APEX-seq full-transcript training. Its reported cross-cell nuclear/cytoplasmic transfer is informative, but assigning a short reporter insert to a complete transcript segment changes input semantics, and transcript localization accuracy does not validate small-edit effects. Public inference scripts and models exist; no explicit repository software license was observed. [Primary RNA-GPS paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7297119/), [official inference code](https://github.com/wukevin/rnagps). Do not treat classifier probability differences as calibrated edit-benefit probabilities.

**Assumption:** a frozen representation retains mutation-sensitive transferable features absent from the explicit sequence model. **Falsification:** no gain beyond a matched local k-mer head, gains vanish with parent exclusion, predictions are effectively constant for SNPs, or improvement requires test-informed windows/layers. Check pretraining sequence overlap separately from supervised leakage. Larger models are not automatically higher priority for a CPU-only, no-cost project.

## Route 5 — Endpoint conditioning and separating localization from stability

**Priority: a fifth prespecified development route is implemented.** A representation can have explicit endpoint-conditioned utility rather than pool all destinations as interchangeable. The implementation uses a prospectively known binary endpoint family (projection or nuclear/cytoplasmic), two separate linear 246-feature pair-RMS heads, and three fixed L2 penalties (.005/.05/.5) selected by the same source-only nested procedure. It never learns a held-out study identity or a head from held-out endpoint labels.

The nuclear head additionally admits exactly 223 previously exposed, candidate-eligible SIRLOIN C1/C2 substitutions in the historical canonical inventory: 162 Jpx_9 rows and 61 NICN1_53 rows, covering two parents and two biological components. These are auxiliary training rows only, never an evaluation set. Their existing exposure and admission statuses are explicitly checked. This expands endpoint-compatible training, so its contrast with the original core-only model mixes endpoint separation and auxiliary-data access; it cannot attribute any gain solely to conditioning. No reserved SIRLOIN outcome is opened. The previously exposed auxiliary outcomes remain unsuitable for a new independent confirmation claim.

Before fitting, the route prepares a frozen core table containing only intervention ID, biological component, parent sequence, and mutant sequence. At each outer and inner fit, every core ID absent from the supplied training frame defines an excluded context. Any auxiliary component sharing one of those core components or any exact parent/mutant allele is removed in its entirety. The fit reads only this metadata table and the admitted auxiliary training outcomes, never excluded core outcomes. Auxiliary file hashes and exclusions are recorded in each fitted model. Five synthetic tests verify outer/inner exclusions, sibling-component purging, label-independent exclusion, known endpoint encoding, and explicit dispatch.

The two auxiliary parents provide limited nuclear diversity, and transferring a model learned from longer SIRLOIN inserts to the short SRLE core remains a substantial covariate extrapolation. Improved compatible-endpoint prediction would support an engineering result under these data, not a universal shared localization mechanism or a calibrated edit-benefit probability. Additional same-sequence cross-cell tests can be informative, but each matched parent/gene must be excluded from all training sources to establish sequence generalization.

RNA stability can influence distal localization: primary cortical and stem-cell-derived neuron experiments show associations and perturbation effects involving half-life, m6A, and destabilizing elements. [Loedige et al., Molecular Cell 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1097276523004689). Mikl's reporter study includes both localization and stability analyses and two neuronal cell lines; these are distinct channels and should be admitted only with a verified source map. [Mikl et al., Nucleic Acids Research 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9561380/), [official GSE173098 design](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE173098).

**Assumption:** some failed transfer comes from mixing transport, retention, expression, and survival mechanisms. **Falsification:** endpoint stratification does not improve compatible-domain transfer; source-trained stability proxies explain apparent gains completely; or cross-cell effects fail on excluded sequences/genes. Localization ratio prediction and specific active transport must remain different claims unless independent channels support the latter. Stability outcomes reserved for confirmation remain protected; this route does not authorize opening them. A same-sequence cross-cell evaluation must remove that sequence/gene from all training sources, otherwise it measures cell adaptation of a familiar sequence rather than sequence generalization.

## Availability and claims

The current feature routes require only admitted sequences and existing numerical libraries. No data download, installation, purchase, model fitting, or protected-outcome access was performed for this literature memo. The feature module's synthetic tests used bundled Codex Python.

Publicly accessible resources verified from authoritative sources include GSE173098, GSE330741, the eLife study's GSE200004 link, RNA-FM code/checkpoint links, ViennaRNA code, and DeepRiPe code (GPL-3.0). [Official DeepRiPe repository/license](https://github.com/ohlerlab/DeepRiPe), [primary DeepRiPe paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7050519/). DeepRiPe may provide an optional frozen binding-effect representation, but exact checkpoint availability and compatibility require separate verification before use. Public code availability is not itself a permissive redistribution license. An endogenous transcriptome resource such as GSE200004 can evaluate transcript localization transfer but cannot by itself validate a mutant-minus-parent small-edit claim.

General RNA localization prediction, transferable motifs, context-specific localization, and mutational effects are already established research topics. Novelty must be evaluated for the actual resulting method, hypothesis, controlled benchmark, and finding. A positive development score on these repeatedly exposed studies is insufficient to establish a novel biological mechanism or prospective generalization.
