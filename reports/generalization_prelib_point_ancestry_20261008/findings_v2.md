# PRELib point-edit source feasibility, 8 October 2026

The outcome-free audit reconstructs4054 literal point edits across16 WT/mPRE template backgrounds, eight native fragments and seven nominal RNA lineages. Every candidate matches the entire140nt baseline with exactly its declared reference/alternate substitution. Source-ID coordinates are zero-based: the original one-based audit flagged all4054; that original source and result remain preserved, and a separately frozen v2 passes all4054 with nine invented tests.

This is a usable lead for auxiliary binding supervision, not a training admission or a localization result. Parent pointers are reconstructed from literal source-ID hierarchy and exact whole-template cross-table sequence matches. Fourteen baselines match an exact TableS2 ID footprint; the two engineered mPRE ENCODE backgrounds have exact TableS3 template matches. Mature RNA boundaries, independent genomic lineage mapping and numerical outcome-row processing have not been certified.

Preserved source problems: TableS3 reports6294 oligos while TableS4 supplies6293 rows. This is a one-row count discrepancy; the identity of any absent construct is unknown. TableS4 has6244 uniqueIDs:49 IDs each label two distinct sequences, all in the kmer branch. Point parents and candidates have uniqueIDs. No source rows were deduplicated or changed. Source gene metadata supports IRF2BP1 despite the Results prose's IGF2BP1. Keep the literal Hafner_APIS1 prefix; AP1S1 is the matching nominal gene from the other author metadata. TableS3's mPRE NORAD NRU3 baseline row repeats the WT sequence, whereas its mPRE mutation-group rows contain the engineered template; both are retained.

TableS2 has two sequences with lowercase bases; TableS3 has ten intentionally absent baseline-sequence cells. No case changes, imputation, padding or invented sequence occurred. TableS1's one total formula was retained as unevaluated/null, and its original rejection is preserved.

All binding/repression/l2fc/raw/normalized numerical values remain outside this work. Source workbook XML/shared-string bytes were loaded internally, but only frozen allowlisted metadata fields were decoded. No parents were chosen by sequence distance, no supervised pair or model was admitted, and no untreated external localization cohort was created.
