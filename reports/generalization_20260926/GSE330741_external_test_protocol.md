## Test A: exact source predictor and mathematical compatibility

Primary model is **srle_2mer_full**, using the unique original-sequence-holdout SRLE 2-mer model and composition fallback in `results/srle_prediction_20260926/fitted_parameters.json`, partition `{scheme: sequence_holdout, held_group: original_hash}`. The primary feature class is chosen from the already-known SRLE strict-purge result; no target outcome selects a model or one of the 70 group-specific fits. The original training partition supplies a single preexisting deployable parameter vector. The strict-purge 10.19% result assessed 70 different held-group fits, so the external predictor must not be misrepresented as one uniquely identified “10.19% weight vector.”

For a parent P and mutant M, use the **unchanged overlapping count definition** for the supplied full insert, `Δx=counts(M)−counts(P)`. Counts are lexicographic A/C/G/T 1-, 2-, 3-mers exactly as in the original function. No window length, anchoring, motif or new feature is chosen from target outcomes. Equal-length unchanged sequence cancels, so the SNV difference affects only its adjacent words. Absolute 190-nt counts lie outside the source's six-mer support, but the feature dimension and linear difference are mathematically defined. No arbitrary sliding-window averaging is required. Boundary SNPs use only available insert bases; no flanking reporter sequence is imputed.

The full source prediction is `Δmono · (composition_coefficient/scale) + Δ2mer · (2mer_residual_coefficient/scale)`. All 190-nt compositions are unseen in the six-mer source, so both endpoints use the **already-frozen composition fallback**. Its intercept, order intercept and scaler centering cancel in differences. Composition changes cannot be discarded as in SRLE swaps. This is extrapolation to another length, mutation class, species, cell type and endpoint, not mathematical proof of transportability.

The source-to-target sign is **+1**, frozen now: source-positive nuclear-retention predictions are tested for positive target SN/cortex edit effects. This is an explicit empirical parameter-transfer hypothesis, not an assertion that the compartments are equivalent. No sign flip, calibration, target centering learned from outcomes or alternate interpretation will rescue a negative result. Negative correlation stays negative. Both requested selection directions are evaluated from this one fixed orientation.

## Primary metric, statistical units and frozen criteria

**One A primary quantitative metric: unweighted mean of per-parent signed Spearman correlations** between frozen prediction and WT-subtracted localization effect. It is scale-insensitive and directly measures ranking throughout each candidate set. For a constant prediction, report operational rank skill 0 with an explicit “constant prediction / undefined mathematical correlation” flag. A constant observed target stops the ranking branch; do not silently discard a parent. All admitted parents remain separately reported.

Uncertainty uses **10,000 fixed overlap-component bootstrap resamples**, seed 20260926, retaining all SNPs/parents within each component. The point estimate gives each parent equal weight; bootstrap samples carry each sampled component's parent count, so means are ratios of parent sums to parent counts. With seven parents/five components/two genes, intervals are descriptive and small-sample-limited. Exact one-sided block sign flips of parent-aggregated contrasts enumerate all 2^5 component sign assignments, never thousands of SNPs as independent units. Required baseline contrasts are an intersection-union decision: every contrast must pass, not a minimum p-value winner. Exploratory secondary tables are not new confirmatory tests.

A **bounded strong-success** classification requires: primary mean rho >0 and bootstrap lower bound >0; paired rho advantage with lower bound >0 and one-sided block-signflip p≤0.05 against each of no change, source composition, source ΔAU and source substitution baseline; candidate regret improvement versus uniform ≥0.02 with bootstrap lower bound >0; more than half the parents improving; no increase in wrong-direction choice versus uniform. Short-k-mer secondary comparisons are reported and may limit any claim of specific 2-mer superiority. Success remains qualified by partial historical exposure and small biological breadth. An uncertain positive is partial signal; absent/negative primary or failed superiority is not strong transfer. No criterion is revised after reveal.

Secondary: per-parent Pearson, sign accuracy, MAE/RMSE/MSE reduction versus no change (uncalibrated cross-endpoint diagnostics), full prediction/observed scatter, per-parent candidate regret, direction, wrong-direction rate, exact best recovery, top-5 best-in-shortlist recovery, and fraction of parents beating each comparator. No significance filtering. Exact/tolerance zero rules: 1e-12 for signs; deterministic prediction ties lexical element ID; measured tied best sets all count as best. Both increase and decrease are evaluated; normalised regret is `(best signed effect − selected signed effect)/signed-effect range`. Uniform is the exact candidate-wise average, including sign outcomes. No unchanged-parent or abstention option is added.

## Test B: independent-assay method study, not zero-shot confirmation

Run B only if A does not meet all strong-success conditions. If A strongly succeeds, stop model development and perform only locked verification/reporting. If A is partial or fails, preserve it before B. Target outcomes now train B, so B is explicitly **independent-assay method replication**, not untouched external or zero-shot validation. Prior partial exposure and two-gene scope still apply.

Primary outer folds hold out each complete parent and all its SNPs, **also removing every overlapping same-gene parent interval from training**. This avoids sharing the same UTR positions between train and test. Original held-parent outcomes never enter scaling, fitting or alpha selection. Inner validation repeats the same whole-parent/overlap exclusion using only remaining training parents.

All learned models are StandardScaler + Ridge, with alpha in exactly **[1,10,100]**, chosen by mean inner held-parent MSE; ties within 1e-12 prefer the larger alpha. No seed search. Sample weights give training parents equal total weight while summing to the number of rows. An intercept is fitted. Every learned model also includes centered training-parent nuisance indicators; held parents receive zeros, so their identity or mean response is never supplied. These indicators are centered on training frequencies and their unknown-parent contribution is zero before scaling. Their use cannot confer known held-parent effects.

Models: no change; training-parent-balanced mean; ΔAU; Δ1-mer; 12-way substitution identity; position only (normalized position, its square, first/last flag); substitution+composition; substitution+position; **simple_full = substitution+composition+position**; isolated Δ2-mer; isolated Δ3-mer; Δ1/2/3-mer; **simple_full+Δ2-mer (B primary)**; simple_full+Δ2-mer+Δ3-mer. Composition is mathematically contained in substitution identity; redundant blocks alter Ridge regularization, not information, and this is disclosed. No neural or positional-pair architecture is added.

B primary claim tests the **paired mean-parent rho increment of simple_full+Δ2-mer over simple_full**, with the same overlap-component uncertainty and fixed sign-flip calculation. Require positive estimate/lower bound and one-sided p≤0.05 plus candidate-regret improvement ≥0.02 with lower bound >0 against both simple_full and uniform, majority parents improved, and no increase in wrong-direction selection. All other named models and baseline contrasts remain visible; failure of a stronger comparator limits interpretation. Quantitative MSE reductions are secondary with parent-balanced means. The nested comparison asks whether adjacency adds beyond substitution, composition, position and training-parent nuisance structure.

A prespecified secondary **leave-one-gene-out** check uses only simple_full and simple_full+Δ2-mer, fixed alpha10, training on the other gene, no target tuning. There are only two folds; report them descriptively without pretending two genes yield a population confidence interval. No random SNP split is run.
