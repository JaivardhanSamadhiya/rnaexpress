# Whole-site accessibility: fixed observed-data follow-up

This additive generation tests whether a global RNA ensemble's probability that an **entire motif interval is unpaired** adds held-assay candidate-edit ranking information beyond the existing arithmetic mean of constituent per-base unpaired marginals. The four repeatedly exposed assays remain development data. No new method, independent confirmation, causal mechanism, physical occupancy or localization probability is claimed. Old verdicts and protocols remain unchanged.
